"Is a digital signature valid in India?" has three answers, because the phrase covers three different things. The Information Technology Act, 2000 recognises **electronic signatures** as equivalent to handwritten ones — but only certain kinds, for certain documents.

## The three kinds

| | DSC (USB token) | Aadhaar eSign | Signature image / drawn |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | Certificate on a crypto token issued by a licensed CA (eMudhra, Sify, Capricorn…) | One-time signature via Aadhaar OTP/biometric through an ESP | A PNG of your signature placed on a PDF |
| Legal status | Secure electronic signature under IT Act s.3 | Electronic signature under s.3A (Second Schedule) | Not a recognised e-signature; evidentiary value only |
| Identity assurance | High (in-person/video KYC) | High (Aadhaar) | None |
| Cost | ₹1,000–2,500 for 2 years (Class 3) | ₹5–30 per signature via platforms; free on some govt portals | Free |
| Needed for | MCA/ROC filings, GST (companies/LLPs), income-tax audit filings, e-tenders, IEC, patents | Contracts, NDAs, offer letters, invoices, loan agreements, rent agreements | Internal approvals, quotations, letters where nobody will dispute authorship |

## When each is *required*

### DSC (Class 3)
- Every MCA filing by a company/LLP director (DIN-linked).
- GST returns and registration for companies and LLPs (proprietors can use EVC/OTP instead).
- Income-tax returns where audit is applicable (ITR-3/5/6 with audit), and all company returns.
- Government e-procurement (GeM bids above thresholds, state tenders), DGFT/IEC, trademark e-filing by agents.
- Signing e-invoices via certain integrations (though IRP-signed invoices don't need your DSC).

Since 2021 only **Class 3** is issued; Class 2 was discontinued. Get one with **signing + encryption** if you bid on tenders.

### Aadhaar eSign
Good enough — and legally solid — for almost everything commercial:
- Employment contracts, offer/experience letters, NDAs
- Vendor agreements, service contracts, SOWs
- Rent and leave-and-licence agreements (registration still needed where the state requires it)
- Loan and KYC documents (banks use it daily)

Platforms: Leegality, SignDesk, Digio, Zoho Sign, DocuSign (with Aadhaar), and the government's own eSign on DigiLocker for many forms.

### Signature image
Fine for documents where the question "did you really sign this?" will never be litigated — quotations, internal approvals, thank-you letters, a stamped invoice copy. Draw or type one with the [digital signature maker](/tools/digital-signature) and place the transparent PNG on the [letterhead](/tools/letterhead), [quotations](/tools/service-quote) or [HR letters](/tools/experience-letter). Just know it carries no more weight than a scanned signature.

## What the IT Act excludes

Electronic signatures of any kind are **not valid** for: negotiable instruments (except cheques), powers of attorney, trusts, wills, and contracts for sale of immovable property. Those still need wet ink (and often registration).

## Stamp duty still applies

An eSigned agreement still needs the correct stamp duty; most eSign platforms integrate e-stamping (SHCIL/state portals). An unstamped contract is admissible only after paying duty and penalty.

## Practical setup for a small business

1. **Proprietor:** no DSC needed for GST/ITR (use Aadhaar OTP/EVC). Get one only if you bid on tenders.
2. **Company/LLP:** every director/partner who signs filings needs a Class 3 DSC; renew before expiry (filings fail silently otherwise).
3. Use an **eSign platform** for contracts — ₹20 per signature is cheaper than one dispute.
4. Keep a **signature PNG** for everyday documents; never share the file publicly.
5. Store signed PDFs with their audit trail (eSign platforms provide it) for the document's retention period.

## FAQ

### Is a WhatsApp "OK" a valid acceptance of a contract?
Courts have treated messages as evidence of agreement in some cases, but it is weak proof. Use eSign for anything with money attached.

### Can I use DocuSign without Aadhaar?
Yes; "simple" e-signatures are valid under Section 10A of the IT Act between consenting parties, but Aadhaar eSign or DSC gives stronger evidentiary presumption.

### How long does a DSC take?
Same day with video KYC from most CAs; the token is couriered or downloaded to a token you already own.

### Is a signature typed in a cursive font legally a signature?
Only as much as any image — it shows intent but doesn't authenticate identity. Use it for low-stakes documents.
