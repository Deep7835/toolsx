"MSME loan scheme" is a perennial top search because the schemes are real, the money is cheap, and the process is confusing. This guide covers the five that matter for a shop, service business or small manufacturer, with 2026 limits.

## Quick comparison

| Scheme | Amount | Collateral | Typical rate | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PM Mudra (PMMY)** | Shishu ≤ ₹50k · Kishore ₹50k–5L · Tarun ₹5–10L · Tarun Plus ₹10–20L | None | 9–12% (bank/NBFC) | Non-farm micro units, traders, services |
| **CGTMSE-covered loans** | Up to ₹5 crore (₹10 cr enhanced) | None (guarantee fee 0.37–1.35%) | Bank MSME rate ~9–12% | Udyam-registered manufacturing & services |
| **PMEGP** | Manufacturing ₹50L · Services ₹20L, with **15–35% subsidy** | None up to ₹10L | Bank rate | New units; 8th pass for > ₹10L; one per family |
| **Stand-Up India** | ₹10L – ₹1 crore | CGTMSE cover | Bank rate | SC/ST or woman entrepreneur, greenfield |
| **PM Vishwakarma** | ₹1L then ₹2L | None | **5%** (subsidised) | 18 traditional trades (carpenter, tailor, goldsmith, potter…) |
| **PM SVANidhi** | ₹10k → ₹20k → ₹50k | None | 7% subvention on timely repayment | Street vendors |
| **Udyam Assist / TReDS** | Invoice discounting | Receivable itself | 8–11% | MSEs supplying to large buyers |

## Mudra: the default first loan

Apply at any bank, small finance bank or NBFC, or via **udyamimitra.in**. Documents: identity/address proof, Udyam certificate, business plan/quotation for machinery, six months' bank statements, GST returns if registered. Shishu loans are frequently sanctioned within a week; Tarun Plus (the ₹20 lakh tier added in 2024) requires a clean repayment record on an earlier Mudra loan.

Rate is set by the lender; PSU banks price near MCLR + 1–2%. Processing fee is waived on Shishu. Repayment 3–7 years.

## CGTMSE: collateral-free for real businesses

The Credit Guarantee Fund covers the bank against default, so the bank does not ask for property. Coverage up to ₹5 crore (75–85%); the "enhanced" scheme covers up to ₹10 crore for Udyam-registered units. You pay a small annual guarantee fee (0.37% for loans up to ₹10 lakh, rising with size). Ask specifically: "Can this be covered under CGTMSE?" — branch staff sometimes default to asking for collateral.

## PMEGP: the one with a subsidy

Implemented by KVIC/DICs for **new** units. Margin money (subsidy) of 15% (urban) / 25% (rural) for general category, 25%/35% for special categories, on project cost. Apply on kviconline.gov.in with a project report; interview by the district committee; bank sanctions the loan; subsidy sits with the bank for 3 years and is adjusted if you run the unit. Manufacturing projects up to ₹50 lakh, services up to ₹20 lakh. A second loan for expansion is available for units that repaid the first.

## PM Vishwakarma: 5% loans for artisans

For 18 trades — carpenters, blacksmiths, goldsmiths, potters, cobblers, tailors, masons, barbers, washermen, and more. Register via CSC; get training (with stipend), a ₹15,000 toolkit incentive, then ₹1 lakh collateral-free at 5% (18-month tenure), and ₹2 lakh after repayment. Also digital-transaction incentives.

## What makes applications succeed

1. **Udyam certificate** — mandatory for CGTMSE and priority-sector tagging; free.
2. **Six months of clean bank statements** with business inflows — route UPI collections through the business account.
3. **GST returns** (if registered) that match bank credits.
4. **A one-page plan**: what you'll buy, what it earns, how you'll repay. Use the [profit margin calculator](/tools/profit-margin) for unit economics and the [EMI calculator](/tools/emi-calculator) to show the repayment fits cash flow.
5. **Quotations** for machinery/stock — a [purchase order](/tools/purchase-order) or supplier quotation shows the money's use.
6. **Credit score** above 700 (CIBIL) — or, for a first loan, no defaults on personal borrowings.

## Alternatives when banks say no

- **NBFC digital loans** (Lendingkart, Flexiloans, bank-backed fintechs): faster, 14–24%, based on GST/bank data.
- **Invoice discounting on TReDS** for receivables from large or government buyers — often cheaper than a term loan, and pairs with the [45-day payment rule](/blog/section-43b-h-45-day-payment-rule).
- **Supplier credit** — negotiate 30-day terms; it's interest-free working capital.
- Avoid app-based "instant" personal loans at 30%+ for business needs.

## FAQ

### Can I get a Mudra loan without collateral?
Yes — all Mudra loans are collateral-free by design; banks may ask for a personal guarantee.

### Is there a subsidy on MSME loans?
PMEGP gives 15–35% margin money; interest subvention exists in specific state schemes and for PM SVANidhi/Vishwakarma. Plain Mudra and CGTMSE loans have no subsidy but no collateral.

### How long does sanction take?
Shishu Mudra: 3–10 days. CGTMSE term loans: 2–4 weeks. PMEGP: 2–3 months including training and committee approval.

### Can a trader apply for CGTMSE?
Retail and wholesale trade were brought under CGTMSE coverage in 2022–23 for Udyam-registered traders; check the current guidelines with your bank.
