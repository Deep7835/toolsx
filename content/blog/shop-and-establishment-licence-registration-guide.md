The **Shops and Establishments Act** is a state law that regulates working hours, holidays, wages and conditions in shops, offices and commercial establishments — and requires every such establishment to be **registered** with the local labour department, usually within 30 days of starting. Banks ask for it to open a current account, and it is the "business proof" most other registrations accept. Each state has its own Act, portal and fee, which is why "shop act licence" searches are endless.

## Who must register

Shops, retail stores, offices, warehouses, restaurants, hotels, theatres, clinics, salons, coaching centres, agencies, freelancers running an office, and — in most states — **businesses run from home** that employ anyone. Factories are covered by the Factories Act instead. Several states exempt establishments with **no employees** from the licence but still require an intimation.

## Documents

- PAN of the business/owner; Aadhaar of the owner/partners/directors
- Proof of the premises: rent agreement or ownership proof, plus electricity bill
- Photo of the shop front with signboard (name in the local language where mandated)
- Constitution document: partnership deed / incorporation certificate / MOA
- Details of employees (count, names, weekly off)
- GST certificate (if any); trade licence from the municipal body where required
- Fee payment

Fees and thresholds change with state notifications; the portal shows the current schedule at application.

## How to apply (typical online flow)

1. Register on the state labour portal with mobile/email OTP.
2. Fill the form: establishment name and category, address, nature of business, owner and manager details, employee count by gender, working hours, weekly holiday, date of commencement.
3. Upload documents (PDF/JPEG, size limits apply).
4. Pay the fee online.
5. Download the **registration certificate (Form F / equivalent)** — usually instant to 7 days; some states inspect before issuing.
6. **Display the certificate** at the premises.

Count the 30-day deadline from commencement in calendar days; use the [business days calculator](/tools/business-days) if your state specifies working days.

## What the Act regulates (and inspectors check)

- **Hours:** typically 9 hours/day, 48/week; overtime at double rate; a weekly holiday; rest intervals.
- **Opening/closing times** (relaxed in most states; 24×7 allowed for many categories with conditions).
- **Women's night work** — permitted with consent, transport and security under the OSH Code and state rules.
- **Leave:** earned leave (usually 1 per 20 days worked), casual and sick leave, national and festival holidays.
- **Wages:** paid by the 7th, wage slips issued — the [wage slip generator](/tools/wage-slip) covers the format.
- **Registers:** attendance, wages, leave — most states now accept electronic registers.
- **Displays:** certificate, working hours, weekly holiday, wages notice.

Penalties for non-registration are modest (₹1,000–₹10,000, more for repeat) but it holds up bank accounts, GST verifications and tenders.

## Changes and closure

Notify changes (address, name, employee count) within 15–30 days, usually by amending online. On closure, inform within 15 days and surrender the certificate to avoid renewal demands.

## FAQ

### Is a Shop Act licence needed for an online business run from home?
In most states, yes, if you employ anyone or operate commercially from the premises; some states exempt sole operators. It also serves as business proof for a current account.

### Is it the same as a trade licence?
No. The trade licence is issued by the municipal corporation for specific trades (food, health, etc.); the Shops & Establishments registration is from the labour department. Many businesses need both.

### How long does it take?
Instant to 7 working days in most states with online portals; up to 30 days where inspection precedes issue.

### Do I need to renew?
Depends on the state: Maharashtra (post-2017) and Delhi are lifetime; Karnataka 5 years; Telangana and West Bengal annually.
