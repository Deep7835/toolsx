A trademark is the legal claim on your brand name, logo or tagline. Without it, a competitor — or a marketplace seller copying your listing — can use your name, and you have little recourse. Registration is cheaper than most people think, and the search is free.

## Cost in 2026

| Applicant | Government fee per class (e-filing) |
|---|---|
| Individual, startup (DPIIT), **MSME (Udyam registered)** | **₹4,500** |
| Company, LLP, partnership (non-MSME) | ₹9,000 |
| Professional fees (optional; agent/attorney) | ₹3,000–₹15,000 |

Physical filing costs 10% more. Registration lasts **10 years**, renewable indefinitely (₹9,000 per class). If you have a Udyam certificate, use it — it halves the fee. [Get Udyam first](/blog/new-msme-definition-udyam-2025-benefits).

## Classes: what you are protecting

Trademarks are registered per **class** of goods/services (45 classes under the Nice classification). Common ones:

| Class | Covers |
|---|---|
| 25 | Clothing, footwear |
| 29, 30 | Packaged foods; tea, coffee, snacks, spices |
| 35 | Retail, e-commerce, advertising, business services |
| 41 | Education, coaching, entertainment |
| 42 | Software, SaaS, IT services |
| 43 | Restaurants, cafés, hotels |
| 44 | Salons, clinics, wellness |
| 9 | Apps, electronics |
| 3 | Cosmetics, soaps |

A D2C clothing brand selling online typically files in 25 and 35. Each class is a separate fee.

## Step 0: search (free, do it today)

Go to ipindiaonline.gov.in → Trade Marks → **Public Search**. Search your name (and phonetic variants) in your classes. If an identical or similar mark exists in the same class, pick another name now — before the signboard, domain and packaging. Also check the domain and social handles; the [favicon generator](/tools/favicon-generator) and [letterhead](/tools/letterhead) can wait a day.

## The five stages

### 1. Filing (Form TM-A) — day 1
Online on the IP India portal with a Class 3 DSC (or through an agent). Provide the mark (word or logo image), class(es), goods/services description, applicant details, user date ("proposed to be used" or since a date with proof). You get an **application number the same day** — from that moment you may use **™**.

### 2. Examination — 1 to 6 months
The examiner checks for conflicts and objections (descriptive marks, similar existing marks). If an **examination report** issues, reply within **30 days** with arguments and evidence of use. Most objections on distinctiveness are overcome by showing the mark is not merely descriptive or by disclaiming a common word.

### 3. Publication in the Trade Marks Journal — 4 months window
Accepted marks are advertised. Anyone may **oppose** within four months. Oppositions are uncommon for small brands; if one comes, counter-statement within two months.

### 4. Registration — typically 8 to 18 months from filing
Certificate issued; you may use **®**. Using ® before registration is an offence.

### 5. Renewal — every 10 years
Six months before expiry; a 6-month grace with surcharge.

## Word mark or logo?

A **word mark** (plain text) protects the name in any font or colour and is the stronger, more flexible filing. Register the logo separately if it is distinctive. Start with the word mark.

## After registration

- Add ® to packaging, website footer and [social posts](/tools/social-post); mark your brand in [Organization schema](/tools/schema-markup).
- Enrol in **Amazon Brand Registry** / Flipkart brand protection — a registered (or pending, in some cases) trademark is the entry ticket, and it lets you remove copycat listings.
- Record it with Customs (IPR enforcement) if you import branded goods.
- Watch the journal for confusingly similar filings; oppose early.

## Common mistakes

- Descriptive names ("Best Quality Rice") — refused or weak. Coined or suggestive names register easily.
- Filing in one class only, then expanding into another — file for what you'll sell in 3 years.
- Missing the 30-day examination reply — the application is abandoned.
- Not using the mark for 5 years after registration — vulnerable to cancellation for non-use.

## FAQ

### Can I register a trademark myself without a lawyer?
Yes, online with a DSC. Many small businesses do; an agent helps most at the examination-reply stage.

### How long does trademark registration take?
Straightforward applications: 8–12 months. With objections or opposition: 18–24 months. You can use ™ from day one.

### What is the difference between ™ and ®?
™ signals a claim (pending or unregistered); ® means registered — using it without registration is punishable.

### Does a company name registration (MCA) protect my brand?
No. Incorporation protects the company name against identical company names only; trademark protects the brand across trade.
