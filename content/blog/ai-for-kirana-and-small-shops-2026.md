"AI for small business" is the fastest-growing business search in India, and most of the results are either sales pitches or fantasies. This is a field guide from the counter, not the conference. Every item below is something a one-person business can do this week, mostly free.

## What actually works

### 1. Product photos on white backgrounds
Marketplaces want white backgrounds; customers buy from clean images. On-device AI can cut a product out of a cluttered photo in seconds — the [background remover](/tools/background-remover) runs entirely in the browser, no upload. Shoot on a phone, remove the background, export on white.

### 2. Reading bills and business cards into text
Snap a supplier bill and extract the text with [OCR](/tools/image-ocr); it even picks out GSTIN, amounts and phone numbers. That turns a shoebox of bills into a spreadsheet in an afternoon.

### 3. First drafts of captions, descriptions and offers
Whether you use ChatGPT, Gemini, or a template tool like the [social post generator](/tools/social-post), the win is the first draft. You still add the price and the local flavour. Keep captions inside platform limits with the [word counter](/tools/word-counter).

### 4. Hinglish and regional-language copy
Modern models write natural Hinglish, Marathi, Tamil. Ask for three versions in the customer's language and pick one. This alone doubles engagement for many local businesses.

### 5. Translating supplier catalogues and government notices
Paste a Chinese supplier's spec sheet or a GST circular; ask for a plain-language summary in Hindi. Verify anything with money attached, but the comprehension gain is immediate.

### 6. Replying to reviews
"Write a warm 2-line reply to this 3-star review that acknowledges the wait time" — done. Reply to every review; ranking rewards it.

### 7. Explaining a notice or contract
Upload the document and ask "what does this ask me to do and by when?" Then confirm with your CA. It turns panic into a to-do list.

### 8. Spreadsheet formulas and cleanup
"Give me a formula to compute GST-inclusive price with 5% and 18% columns" — copy, paste, done. Cleaning messy phone-number columns is a two-minute job.

### 9. Voice notes to written orders
Speech-to-text in WhatsApp and Google's keyboard is now good in Indian languages. Customers send voice notes; you get text you can search.

### 10. Basic design
AI-assisted design tools produce a festive offer poster in minutes. Resize for WhatsApp, Instagram and print with the [photo resizer](/tools/photo-resizer).

### 11. FAQ chatbots on your website
A simple bot answering "timings, location, delivery area, return policy" from your own FAQ page handles 60% of inbound questions. Keep it narrow.

### 12. Demand hints from your own sales
Paste 12 months of monthly sales by item and ask which items are seasonal and when to stock up. It won't be a forecast, but it will surface patterns you haven't noticed.

## What wastes money

1. **"AI CRM" subscriptions for a 200-customer business.** A spreadsheet and broadcast lists win.
2. **Fully automated WhatsApp sales bots.** Customers can tell, and they leave. Use bots for FAQs, humans for sales.
3. **AI-generated product images of things you don't sell.** Marketplaces reject them and customers return them.
4. **Paid "AI SEO" content farms.** Google's AI Mode rewards businesses with real reviews, real photos and consistent information — see [our local SEO guide](/blog/google-ai-mode-local-seo-for-indian-businesses-2026).
5. **Letting AI file your GST.** Use it to *understand*; let a human sign.

## Two rules to keep you safe

- **Never paste customer data** (names, phones, Aadhaar, card numbers) into a public AI chat. Under DPDP that is a disclosure. Use on-device tools for anything personal.
- **Verify money and law.** AI is confident and sometimes wrong about rates, dates and sections. Check with the official portal or your CA before you act.

## FAQ

### Is there a free AI tool for product photo background removal?
Yes — the [background remover](/tools/background-remover) here runs on-device with an open-source model; nothing is uploaded.

### Can AI write my GST invoice?
It can draft text, but an invoice needs exact fields and math. Use a purpose-built generator like the [GST invoice tool](/tools/gst-invoice) instead.

### Which AI is best for Hindi?
As of 2026 the major models (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude) all handle Hindi and Hinglish well; Gemini has the broadest Indian-language coverage in Google's own apps.

### Do I need to disclose that content is AI-written?
Not for ordinary marketing. Do not use AI to fabricate reviews or testimonials — that is illegal under consumer-protection rules and gets listings removed.
