Budget 2025 raised the investment and turnover limits that define micro, small and medium enterprises by **2.5× and 2× respectively**, effective **1 April 2025**. Overnight, thousands of "small" companies became "micro", and businesses that had outgrown the scheme became eligible again. "Udyam registration new limit" has been a rising search all year.

## The new thresholds

| Category | Investment in plant & machinery/equipment | Annual turnover |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro** | up to ₹2.5 crore (was ₹1 crore) | up to ₹10 crore (was ₹5 crore) |
| **Small** | up to ₹25 crore (was ₹10 crore) | up to ₹100 crore (was ₹50 crore) |
| **Medium** | up to ₹125 crore (was ₹50 crore) | up to ₹500 crore (was ₹250 crore) |

Both conditions must be met; exceeding either bumps you up a category. Exports are excluded from turnover. Classification is automatic from your ITR and GST data linked to the Udyam portal.

## Why you should register (it's free)

### 1. Protection against late payment — Section 43B(h) and MSMED interest
Buyers must pay registered **micro and small** enterprises within **45 days** (15 if there is no written agreement). Beyond that, the buyer cannot claim the expense as a deduction until paid (Section 43B(h)), and owes you **compound interest at 3× the RBI bank rate** under the MSMED Act. You can file on **MSME Samadhaan** to recover. Compute what you are owed with the [late payment interest calculator](/tools/late-payment-interest). This single benefit changed payment behaviour across Indian supply chains.

### 2. Collateral-free credit
Loans up to ₹5 crore under **CGTMSE** without collateral (₹10 crore in the enhanced scheme for Udyam-registered units), plus **Mudra** loans up to ₹20 lakh. Banks price MSME loans lower — compare EMIs with the [EMI calculator](/tools/emi-calculator).

### 3. Priority-sector lending and interest subvention
Banks must direct a share of lending to MSMEs. Several state schemes give 2–3% interest subsidies to Udyam-registered units.

### 4. Government tenders
GeM and most PSU tenders exempt MSEs from earnest money deposits and reserve a share of procurement (25% for MSEs, with sub-quotas for SC/ST and women-owned units).

### 5. Delayed-payment exemption from TReDS discounting
Registered MSEs can discount receivables from large buyers on TReDS platforms (RXIL, M1xchange, Invoicemart) at bank rates — turning 60-day receivables into cash in 48 hours.

### 6. Subsidies on ISO, patents, trademarks and electricity
Reimbursement of up to 50% for ISO certification, up to ₹1 lakh for patent filing, and electricity-tariff concessions in many states.

### 7. Easier licences and ITR/GST linkage
Udyam is now the reference ID for dozens of state schemes, and its data flows automatically from your PAN and GSTIN — no annual renewal.

## How to register in ten minutes

1. Go to udyamregistration.gov.in (the only official site — ignore paid "agents").
2. Aadhaar of the proprietor/partner/director + OTP.
3. PAN and GSTIN are pulled automatically; enter NIC activity codes for what you do.
4. Bank details, employee count, investment and turnover (self-declared; verified later against ITR).
5. Download the Udyam certificate — a permanent number, no renewal.

Print your Udyam number on invoices and purchase orders. The [GST invoice generator](/tools/gst-invoice) and [PO generator](/tools/purchase-order) have a notes field for it, and buyers' accounts teams increasingly look for it to apply the 45-day rule.

## What changed for businesses that "graduated"

If you crossed the old limits and lost benefits in 2023–24, you are likely back inside the new ones. Log in to the Udyam portal — reclassification is automatic once your FY 2024-25 ITR data syncs, but check that your certificate shows the right category.

## FAQ

### Is Udyam registration mandatory?
No, but without it you cannot claim MSME benefits, and your buyers are not bound by the 45-day payment rule.

### Do traders qualify?
Yes. Wholesale and retail traders have been eligible since July 2021 (for priority-sector lending, though not for all schemes).

### My turnover is ₹8 crore with ₹3 crore of machinery. What am I?
Investment above ₹2.5 crore makes you "small" even though turnover is within the micro limit.

### Does Udyam expire?
No. The number is permanent; classification updates automatically each year from ITR/GST data.
