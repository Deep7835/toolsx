A quotation is a sales document and a legal one at the same time. Written well, it closes the deal and protects you when the client's memory of "what was included" drifts. "Quotation format" is a top business search every month because most templates are just price lists — they miss the parts that matter.

## The nine parts of a good quotation

1. **Header** — your business name, address, GSTIN, phone, email; quotation number and date. The [quotation builder](/tools/service-quote) fills these from your saved profile.
2. **Client details** — name, company, address, GSTIN if B2B (they'll want it for their PO).
3. **Reference / project** — "Interior work, 2BHK, Sector 21" or the client's enquiry reference.
4. **Scope of work** — what exactly is included, in plain sentences. This is the paragraph that ends arguments.
5. **Itemised prices** — description, quantity, unit, rate, amount. Separate **materials** and **labour** for contractors; separate **deliverables** and **revisions** for agencies.
6. **Taxes** — show GST per line or state "plus GST @ 18%". Never leave tax ambiguous; a "₹1,00,000 all-inclusive" quote silently costs you 15% if the client assumes GST is inside.
7. **Validity** — "valid for 15 days"; materials prices move.
8. **Terms** — payment schedule (advance / milestones / on delivery), timeline, what happens on change of scope, warranty, exclusions (civil damage, transport, statutory approvals).
9. **Acceptance** — a line for the client to sign/date, or "reply 'Approved' to this email to accept".

## Example wording that works

**Scope:** "Supply and installation of modular kitchen as per drawing K-02 dated 12 Sep 2026, including 18 mm BWP ply carcass, laminate shutters, SS baskets (3), soft-close hinges. Excludes countertop, plumbing, electrical points and painting."

**Payment:** "40% advance to confirm order; 40% on delivery of material to site; 20% on completion and handover. Payments by UPI/NEFT to the account below."

**Validity & changes:** "Prices valid till 5 October 2026. Any change in scope, material or quantity will be quoted separately before execution."

**Timeline:** "3–4 weeks from receipt of advance and site readiness."

## GST on quotations

A quotation itself has no GST effect — nothing is supplied yet. But state the GST treatment clearly:

- B2B: "plus GST @ 18% (SAC 9954)". Use the [GST calculator](/tools/gst-calculator) to show inclusive totals if the client prefers.
- B2C: many customers prefer an all-inclusive number; show the breakup in small print so the invoice matches.
- Composition dealers: state "GST not applicable — composition taxable person".

When the client accepts and pays an advance, issue a [proforma invoice](/tools/proforma-invoice) or receipt voucher; the [tax invoice](/tools/gst-invoice) comes at supply. See [invoice vs bill vs receipt vs quotation](/blog/invoice-vs-bill-vs-receipt-vs-quotation).

## Pricing structure that improves acceptance

- **Three options** (basic / standard / premium) raise average order value; most clients pick the middle.
- **Round totals** but itemised lines — clients trust detail and remember round numbers.
- **Show the discount** as a line ("Festive discount −₹5,000") rather than a silently lower rate; it anchors the value.
- **Optional add-ons** at the bottom ("Extra coat of paint: ₹6,000") sell themselves.

## Mistakes that cost money

- No validity date — material prices rise, client accepts three months later at the old price.
- "Approx." in prices — legally a quotation with "approx." is not an offer capable of acceptance; use fixed prices with a change clause.
- Missing exclusions — the client assumes transport, taxes and approvals are included.
- No revision limit for creative work — "unlimited revisions" is how agencies lose margin.
- Quoting from WhatsApp chat without a document — unenforceable and unprofessional.

## Follow-up rhythm

Day 2: "Did the quotation reach you? Happy to walk you through it." Day 7: value reminder (a photo of similar work). Day 14: validity reminder. After expiry: re-quote at current prices — not the old ones.

## FAQ

### Is a quotation legally binding?
Once accepted (signature, email, or advance payment), it forms a contract on those terms. Until then it is an offer you can withdraw.

### Should a quotation have GST?
It should state the GST treatment; it doesn't create GST liability. Tax is charged on the invoice at supply.

### What is the difference between an estimate and a quotation?
An estimate is an approximate figure that may change; a quotation is a fixed offer. Use "quotation" when you are ready to commit to a price.

### Can I revise a quotation after sending it?
Yes, before acceptance — issue a new version with a new number and date, and withdraw the old one in writing.
