"GST notice fake or real" is a query that spikes every quarter-end, and the department's own advisories confirm why: fraudsters are impersonating tax officers at scale. Small businesses are the target because they are more likely to pay a "penalty" over the phone than to call a CA. Here is how the current scams work and how to be un-scammable.

## The scams doing the rounds

### 1. The "final notice" phone call
A caller with a convincing Hindi/English script says a GSTR-3B mismatch has led to a penalty; pay ₹8,000 by UPI within the hour to avoid registration cancellation. They quote your real GSTIN (public data) to sound legitimate. **Real officers never call to demand payment by UPI.**

### 2. Forged DRC-01/ASMT-10 notices on WhatsApp
A PDF that looks like a show-cause notice, with a QR code that leads to a UPI payment or a fake portal. Real notices are issued **only on the GST portal** under "View Notices and Orders", and every genuine document carries a **DIN (Document Identification Number)** verifiable at the CBIC DIN portal.

### 3. Portal phishing
An SMS: "Your GST return is pending, log in at gst-portal-verify.in". The fake page steals your username, password and then the OTP. The only portal is **gst.gov.in**.

### 4. "Udyam / MSME renewal" fees
Udyam registration is free and permanent. Any "renewal", "certificate" or "MSME award" fee is a scam.

### 5. Fake input tax credit offers
"Buy invoices" from a shell company to claim ITC. This one is not a scam *against* you — it makes you the criminal. Since IMS, the department sees every such invoice; arrests under Section 132 happen monthly.

### 6. Refund scams
"Your GST refund of ₹42,180 is approved, share bank OTP to receive." Refunds credit to the bank account registered on the portal; nobody needs an OTP from you.

## Verify any notice in two minutes

1. Log in to **gst.gov.in** → Services → User Services → **View Notices and Orders**. If it isn't there, it isn't real.
2. Check the **DIN** on the CBIC website ("Verify CBIC-DIN").
3. Check the officer's jurisdiction on your registration certificate; a Delhi officer does not issue notices to a Chennai GSTIN.
4. Never pay tax anywhere except via a **challan (PMT-06) generated on the portal**.

## The 6-point defence

1. **Unique, long passwords** for GST, income-tax, Udyam, bank and email — generate them with the [password generator](/tools/password-generator) and check anything you already use with the [strength checker](/tools/password-strength). Password reuse is how one leak becomes five.
2. **Two-factor authentication** on the GST portal (mandatory for turnover above ₹20 crore, optional and wise for everyone), on email, and on your bank.
3. **Separate the OTP phone.** Keep the number registered on the GST/bank portals on a phone that isn't used for random downloads.
4. **Authorised signatory hygiene.** Remove ex-employees and ex-CAs from the portal's authorised signatory list today.
5. **Scan QR codes before paying.** The [scanner](/tools/barcode-scanner) shows exactly which UPI ID a QR pays — if it isn't a government or known merchant VPA, stop.
6. **A rule for staff:** no one pays any government demand without the owner seeing it on the portal.

## If you've already paid

- Call **1930** (national cyber-crime helpline) immediately and report on cybercrime.gov.in — banks can freeze funds within the first hour.
- Change passwords and revoke sessions on the GST and bank portals.
- Inform your CA; if a fake return or amendment was filed, it must be corrected in the next period.

## FAQ

### Do GST officers ever call taxpayers?
Officers may call to schedule a visit or clarify a filing, but never to demand payment. Any payment demand comes as a portal notice with a DIN.

### Is a notice without DIN valid?
No. CBIC has held that communications without a DIN are invalid, barring narrow exceptions that must be regularised within 15 days.

### Can I receive a genuine notice on email?
You receive an *alert* on the registered email/SMS; the notice itself is on the portal.

### How do I check if a website is the real GST portal?
The address must be exactly https://www.gst.gov.in. Bookmark it and never follow links from SMS.
