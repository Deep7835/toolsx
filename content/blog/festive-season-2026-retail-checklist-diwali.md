The signals are all pointing the same way: iPhone 18 Pro buyers queued and even flew to Dubai for stock, two-wheeler dispatches hit a record in August, and the September 2025 GST cuts on appliances and FMCG mean this is the second festive season with lower shelf prices. Indian retail does 30–40% of its annual sales between Navratri and Diwali. Here is a plan that starts **this week** and runs to **Bhai Dooj on 10 November**.

## Key dates

| Date | Occasion |
|---|---|
| 2 October (Fri) | Gandhi Jayanti — dry day, most markets closed |
| 11–19 October | Navratri / Durga Puja (Puja peaks 17–20 Oct in the east) |
| 15 October | **UPI MDR starts** for merchants above ₹1 lakh/month |
| 20 October (Tue) | Dussehra |
| 6 November (Fri) | Dhanteras — gold, utensils, appliances, vehicles |
| 8 November (Sun) | Diwali / Lakshmi Puja |
| 9 November | Govardhan Puja / Annakut |
| 10 November | Bhai Dooj |
| 15 November | Chhath (Bihar, Jharkhand, east UP) |

Marketplace festive sales usually start the weekend before Navratri (around 9–10 October) and run in waves to Diwali.

## Week by week

### Week of 21 September — stock and cash
- Place orders now; suppliers' lead times double from October. Use last year's sales by SKU — if you don't have them, start recording this year.
- Arrange working capital: a limit on your current account beats a personal loan. Udyam-registered? CGTMSE-backed loans are collateral-free — see [MSME benefits](/blog/new-msme-definition-udyam-2025-benefits).
- **Bank strike 28–30 September**: move salary and supplier payments to Friday the 25th — [details](/blog/bank-strike-28-30-september-2026-keep-your-business-running).

### Week of 28 September — pricing and tags
- Reprice on current GST slabs (5/18/40%); check the [HSN finder](/tools/hsn-finder) for anything you're unsure about.
- Print price tags with MRP, offer price and discount % in bulk from the [price tag generator](/tools/price-tag-generator). Customers buy faster when prices are visible.
- Decide your festive offers: bundles and freebies protect margin better than flat discounts. Model them in the [profit margin calculator](/tools/profit-margin).

### Week of 5 October — payments and staff
- **UPI**: print a fresh standee ([tool](/tools/upi-standee)) for small tickets, and enable dynamic QRs on invoices for large ones. If your UPI inflows exceed ₹1 lakh a month, budget for 0.4% MDR on bills above ₹2,000 from the 15th — [explainer](/blog/upi-mdr-from-15-october-npci-circular-explained).
- Hire festive temps now; issue appointment letters (mandatory under the Labour Codes) and set wages. The [team payroll calculator](/tools/team-payroll) handles part-month pay and the new ₹25,000 PF ceiling.
- Extend hours; women may work night shifts with consent and safeguards.

### Week of 12 October — marketing
- WhatsApp: message only opted-in customers, and only with what they'd want (new stock, Dhanteras timings). Use a [click-to-chat link](/tools/whatsapp-direct) on every poster so customers start the conversation. TRAI's new [anti-spam rules](/blog/trai-anti-spam-rules-2026-businesses-that-call-or-message-customers) apply to calls and SMS.
- Instagram/Facebook: three posts a week, product-first, price included. The [social post generator](/tools/social-post) drafts festival captions in Hinglish.
- Google Business Profile: update holiday hours, add photos of festive stock, ask for reviews with a QR at the counter — [review builder](/tools/google-review).

### Week of 19 October — marketplaces and delivery
- Amazon/Flipkart/Meesho: stock into FBA/Smart warehouses by their cut-off dates (typically 7–10 days before a sale event). Check fee changes on your seller dashboard; reprice with the [seller pricing workflow](/tools/seller-pricing).
- Local delivery: a bike and a WhatsApp number beats a dark store within 2 km. Print [4×6 labels](/tools/shipping-label) for self-ship orders.
- Check packaging stock — boxes, tape, gift wrap — before the 25th.

### Week of 26 October — Dhanteras readiness
- Jewellers and utensil sellers: hallmarking (HUID) is mandatory on gold; with gold near ₹1.5 lakh per 10 g, display making charges and buy-back terms clearly.
- Appliance and electronics dealers: exchange offers and EMI tie-ups drive Dhanteras; ensure the finance partner's app works at your counter.
- Restock fast-movers; don't over-order slow SKUs — post-Diwali returns and dead stock hurt more than a stock-out.

### Week of 2 November — the run-in
- Cash float for change, but push UPI: it's faster at a crowded counter.
- Daily closing: reconcile UPI settlements against bills; MDR deductions will show for the first time.
- Staff rota with rest days; a tired cashier makes expensive mistakes.

### After Diwali
- Returns policy on display (a [refund policy](/tools/refund-policy) page/board avoids arguments).
- File October GSTR-1 by 11 November — festive sales mean bigger tax outflow on 20 November; keep the cash.
- Send a thank-you message with a review request to festive customers in the week after.

## The three mistakes that recur every year

1. **Stock bought on credit without a plan to pay by 45 days** — suppliers who are MSEs can charge 3× bank-rate interest ([Section 43B(h)](/blog/section-43b-h-45-day-payment-rule)).
2. **Discounts announced before margins were computed.**
3. **No record of what sold** — so next year's ordering is a guess again. Even a spreadsheet of daily sales by category pays for itself.

## FAQ

### When do festive sales actually peak?
Two peaks: the weekend before Navratri (online sale events) and the five days from Dhanteras to Bhai Dooj (offline).

### Should I raise prices during the festival?
Marketplaces penalise sudden MRP changes; offline, customers compare with apps. Compete on availability, bundles and service, not on price hikes.

### Is Diwali bonus mandatory?
Statutory bonus applies to employees earning up to ₹21,000 (8.33–20%, computed on ₹7,000 or minimum wage) and must be paid within 8 months of the financial year — [calculator](/tools/statutory-bonus). A festive advance or gift is customary but not mandatory.

### How do I handle the MDR on big-ticket festive purchases?
Absorb it and price for it; it's 0.4% capped at ₹300. Surcharging customers is prohibited.
