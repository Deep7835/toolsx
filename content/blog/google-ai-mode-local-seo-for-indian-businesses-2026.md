Google launched **AI Mode** in India in 2025 — in English and Hindi — and **AI Overviews** now appear on the majority of informational queries. "How to rank in AI Overviews" and "GEO (generative engine optimisation)" are the fastest-growing marketing searches of the year. For a local business the good news is that the fundamentals still decide who gets cited; the bad news is that sloppy listings are invisible faster than ever.

## What AI search actually cites for local queries

When someone asks "best CA for GST filing in Indore" or "electrician near me open now", the AI answer draws from:

1. **Google Business Profile** — hours, categories, services, photos, Q&A, and above all reviews.
2. **Structured data** on your website (LocalBusiness, Product, FAQ schema).
3. **Consistent information** across your site, GBP, Justdial, IndiaMART, Instagram.
4. **Content that directly answers questions** — short, factual, with numbers.
5. **Recency** — profiles and pages updated in the last few months.

It does *not* care about keyword density, "SEO articles" of 2,000 words, or backlinks from directories.

## The five signals that matter now

### 1. Reviews with specifics
"Great service" does nothing. "Got my GST registration done in 3 days, ₹1,500, very responsive on WhatsApp" gets quoted. Ask customers for specifics; the [review request builder](/tools/google-review) drafts a message that prompts details and includes your review link and QR.

### 2. Complete, current Google Business Profile
Every field filled: services with prices, products, hours including holidays, attributes (UPI accepted, wheelchair access), and 5+ new photos a month. Answer every review and every Q&A within 48 hours.

### 3. Schema markup on your site
Add **LocalBusiness** (with `openingHours`, `priceRange`, `geo`), **Product** for what you sell, and **FAQPage** for your common questions. The [schema generator](/tools/schema-markup) and [FAQ schema tool](/tools/faq-schema) produce valid JSON-LD to paste into your page head.

### 4. Titles and descriptions that read like answers
AI systems lean on your title tag and meta description to decide relevance. "Sharma Electricals — Electrician in Kothrud, Pune · 30-min response · UPI" beats "Home | Sharma Electricals". Draft and preview with the [meta tag generator](/tools/meta-tags) and [SERP preview](/tools/serp-preview).

### 5. One page per service, in plain language
A page titled "AC repair in Kothrud — ₹399 visit charge, same-day" with a price table, area list and FAQ is exactly the shape AI answers extract from. Ten such pages beat one long "services" page.

## What to stop doing

- Buying reviews (Google's 2025–26 crackdown removes them and suspends profiles).
- Stuffing city names into every sentence.
- Posting AI-written blog posts with no local information; they don't get cited.
- Changing your business name on GBP to include keywords — that's a suspension trigger.

## A monthly routine (60 minutes)

| Week | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | Post 5 new photos and 1 update on GBP; check hours for upcoming holidays |
| 2 | Ask 10 recent customers for reviews with the QR/link; reply to all reviews |
| 3 | Add or refresh one service page with prices and an FAQ; update schema |
| 4 | Search your top 5 queries in AI Mode; note who's cited and what they say that you don't |

## FAQ

### Does AI Mode replace the map pack?
No — the local map results still appear, and AI answers usually cite businesses from it. A strong GBP feeds both.

### Do I need a website if I have a good Google Business Profile?
For hyperlocal services, GBP alone can work. A one-page site with schema and prices noticeably increases citations.

### How long until changes show up?
GBP edits reflect within days; website schema and content typically within 2–6 weeks.

### Is Hindi content useful for AI search?
Yes. AI Mode serves Hindi answers; a Hindi FAQ on your page can capture queries English pages miss.
