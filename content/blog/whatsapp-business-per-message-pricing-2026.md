WhatsApp is the front desk of Indian small business, and Meta's pricing changes on **1 July 2025** made "WhatsApp Business API cost" a permanent top search. The short version: if you use the free **WhatsApp Business app**, nothing changed. If you use the **API** through a provider (Interakt, Wati, AiSensy, Gupshup, Zoho…), you now pay **per message delivered**, by category.

## The 2026 price sheet for India (approximate)

| Message category | Price per message | When it's free |
|---|---|---|
| **Service** (replies to customers) | Free | Always, within the 24-hour customer-service window |
| **Utility** (order updates, invoices, OTP-style alerts) | ≈ ₹0.10–0.12 | Free when sent inside an open 24-hour window |
| **Authentication** (OTPs) | ≈ ₹0.10 | — |
| **Marketing** (offers, promos, re-engagement) | ≈ ₹0.78–0.88 | Never |

Prices are set by Meta and vary slightly by provider mark-up; volume tiers reduce them. GST at 18% applies.

## Where the money leaks

1. **Templates mis-categorised as marketing.** "Your order is ready — also check our new arrivals!" is marketing, at 8× the utility price. Keep utility templates purely transactional.
2. **Sending utility messages *outside* the 24-hour window.** A "payment received" message 26 hours after the customer last wrote to you is billed; the same message at hour 23 is free. Reply promptly.
3. **Blast campaigns to cold lists.** 5,000 marketing messages ≈ ₹4,400. Fine if it converts; expensive if it's a weekly habit. Meta also throttles marketing to users who ignore messages.
4. **Provider platform fees** on top of Meta's rates — ₹1,000–5,000/month is typical. Compare before renewing.

## How to run near-zero WhatsApp costs

### Use click-to-chat links and QR codes for inbound
Every conversation the *customer* starts opens a free 24-hour window. Put a **wa.me link with a pre-filled message** on your website, Instagram bio, packaging and counter. Generate one with the [WhatsApp direct chat tool](/tools/whatsapp-direct) — customers don't need to save your number.

### Answer within the window
Set expectations ("we reply within 2 hours, 10am–8pm") and use quick replies. Every reply inside the window is free.

### Send invoices and receipts as PDFs in the same window
When a customer orders on chat, send the [payment receipt](/tools/payment-receipt) or invoice PDF *in that conversation*. Free, and it reads as service.

### Ask for reviews inside the window
A review request right after a purchase is a service message. The [Google review request builder](/tools/google-review) writes a polite one with your review link.

### Keep marketing to opted-in, engaged customers
Segment by last purchase (30/90 days). A marketing message to someone who bought last week converts 5–10×; to a 6-month-old lead it mostly gets you blocked. DPDP also requires explicit opt-in for marketing — see the [DPDP checklist](/blog/dpdp-rules-2025-checklist-for-small-websites).

### Use broadcast lists in the free app for micro-lists
The free WhatsApp Business app allows broadcast lists of 256 saved contacts who have your number saved. For a neighbourhood store, that covers the regulars at zero cost.

## App vs API: which do you need?

| | WhatsApp Business app | WhatsApp Business API |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | Per message + provider fee |
| Users | 1 phone (multi-device up to 4 linked) | Unlimited agents |
| Automation | Away messages, quick replies, catalogue | Chatbots, CRM integration, templates |
| Green tick | Rare | Available with verification |
| Best for | Shops, clinics, freelancers | Brands doing 1,000+ chats a month |

Most businesses under ₹5 crore are better served by the free app plus disciplined use of links and QR codes.

## FAQ

### Are replies to customers really free on the API?
Yes — any message you send within 24 hours of the customer's last message is a free service message, regardless of content, except template messages categorised as marketing.

### Can I send a promo as a "utility" template to save money?
Meta reviews templates and reclassifies them; repeated misuse gets templates rejected and can get the number restricted.

### Does the free app have any per-message charge?
No. It is free to use; you pay only for your data plan.

### How much does a green tick cost?
Meta Verified for Business is a paid subscription (from roughly ₹600–1,000/month); the legacy Official Business Account badge is free but granted selectively to notable brands.
