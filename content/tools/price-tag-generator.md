Price tags are the quietest salespeople in the shop and the first thing a Legal Metrology inspector reads. A good tag shows the MRP, the offer price, the saving, the size or weight, and a code the counter can scan — printed in a grid so a rack of 60 items is labelled in ten minutes. This generator lays out **price stickers and shelf tags** in standard label sizes (A4 sheets of 24/40/65 labels, or 50 × 25 mm thermal rolls) with MRP, sale price, discount percentage, batch or SKU, barcode and your logo, exported as PDF or PNG from your browser.

## What must appear on a price label

| Element | Rule or reason |
|---|---|
| MRP (inclusive of all taxes) | Mandatory on pre-packaged goods under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules; never sell above it |
| Offer / selling price | May be shown alongside MRP; must not obscure the original MRP on the manufacturer's pack |
| Net quantity | On packaged goods (grams, ml, pieces) |
| Batch / lot, date of packing or manufacture, best before | On packaged food and cosmetics (FSSAI and Legal Metrology) |
| Packer / manufacturer name and address, consumer care contact | Packaged goods |
| Barcode / SKU | Optional but essential for scanning at the counter |
| Country of origin | For imported goods and e-commerce listings |

Loose goods (vegetables, hardware by weight) need a rate per unit displayed, not an MRP. The label checklist for food, including the veg/non-veg logo, is in [FSSAI rules for small food businesses](/blog/fssai-labelling-rules-small-food-business).

## Discounts on tags

The saving line is computed for you: MRP ₹1,299, offer ₹899 → "Save ₹400 (31%)". Stacked or conditional offers ("extra 10% on bills above ₹2,000") belong on a shelf talker, not the sticker; check the effective percentage with the [discount calculator](/tools/discount-calculator) and make sure the counter software applies it the same way. Under Consumer Protection rules, a "was" price must be a price you actually charged, and "up to 50% off" needs a real 50% item in the range.

## GST and the tag

MRP already includes GST, so a tag never shows tax separately for consumers. When the slab on an item changed under GST 2.0, manufacturers were allowed to re-sticker; retailers must sell at the revised MRP shown on the pack. For your own packaged products, compute the inclusive MRP from cost, margin and rate with the [GST calculator](/tools/gst-calculator) and [gross margin calculator](/tools/gross-margin); the reverse arithmetic is in [How to calculate GST from MRP](/blog/gst-on-mrp-inclusive-price-calculation).

## Practical tips

- Use removable adhesive labels for products you may return to suppliers.
- Colour-code offer tags (red for clearance, green for new) so staff can find them at re-pricing time.
- Print the SKU in text too; barcodes get scratched.
- For festive resets, plan the re-tagging day into the [festive season checklist](/blog/festive-season-2026-retail-checklist-diwali).

## FAQ

### Can I print my own MRP label on a manufacturer's product?

No. The MRP on pre-packaged goods is declared by the manufacturer or packer. You may add an offer price sticker beside it but must not cover or alter the original MRP.

### Is it legal to show a discount percentage?

Yes, as long as the "original" price is genuine and the offer terms are clear. Misleading strike-through prices are an unfair trade practice under the Consumer Protection Act.

### What label sheets does the A4 layout fit?

Standard grids sold in India — 24-up (70 × 37 mm), 40-up (52.5 × 29.7 mm), 65-up (38 × 21 mm) — and common thermal roll sizes. Choose the grid that matches your stationery.

### Do I need a barcode on price tags?

Not legally, but a barcode or SKU speeds billing and reduces errors. Code 128 with your own SKU is enough for in-store scanning.

### Can I generate tags for hundreds of items?

Yes. Paste a list (name, MRP, price, SKU) and the generator fills sheets automatically, keeping the list in your browser for reprints.
