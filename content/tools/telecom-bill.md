Small telecom resellers, PBX and cloud-telephony providers, companies that recover mobile expenses from employees, and businesses that re-bill call charges to clients all need a clear itemised bill: plan rental, usage, add-ons, taxes and the due date. This tool lays out a professional **postpaid-style telecom bill in your own business name**, with a summary, itemised charges and GST, and exports it as a PDF. It is designed for billing your own customers or documenting reimbursements — not for reproducing any mobile operator's bill.

## Who bills telecom charges

- **Cloud telephony and IVR vendors** billing SMEs for virtual numbers, call minutes and SMS packs.
- **Corporate mobile plan administrators** recovering personal-usage excess from staff, or allocating costs across departments.
- **Franchise and channel partners** of operators who invoice enterprise customers for bundles.
- **Coworking spaces** that provide desk phones or DID numbers to members.
- **Freelancers and agencies** that pass through call and SMS costs to clients at cost, with a service fee.

## What the bill contains

| Section | Details |
|---|---|
| Account summary | Account number, billing period, bill date, due date, previous balance, payments received, current charges, total due |
| Plan and rental | Monthly plan charge, pro-rated if the plan changed mid-cycle |
| Usage | Voice minutes, SMS, data beyond the bundle, roaming, ISD — with rates |
| Value-added services | Caller tunes, DID numbers, call recording, IVR seats |
| One-time charges | Activation, SIM replacement, late fee |
| Taxes | GST at 18% on telecom services (SAC 9984) |
| Payment options | UPI QR, bank details, payment link |

## How to use the generator

1. Enter your business name, GSTIN and logo, then the customer's name, mobile/account number and billing address with state (for CGST/SGST vs IGST).
2. Set the billing period and dates. Add the plan rental — the tool pro-rates when you enter activation or change dates.
3. Add usage lines with quantity and rate (for example 120 ISD minutes at ₹6.50), add-ons and one-time charges.
4. Apply any credits or the previous balance; GST is computed automatically.
5. Download the PDF or share a summary on WhatsApp with a UPI QR for the total.

## GST on telecom services

Telecommunication services attract **18% GST**. For B2B customers, print their GSTIN so they can claim input credit; the place of supply for postpaid services is the billing address of the customer, so a Delhi vendor billing a Bengaluru company charges IGST. Pre-paid packs are taxed at the point of sale. If you merely recover an operator's charge from an employee, that is not a supply — it is a salary adjustment or reimbursement — and needs a note in payroll rather than a GST invoice; see the [in-hand salary calculator](/tools/in-hand-salary) for how reimbursements interact with taxable pay.

## Regulatory reminders for anyone messaging customers

If your business sends promotional or transactional SMS, TRAI's DLT registration rules apply: register the entity, headers and templates, and honour DND preferences. From 2026 the tightened anti-spam norms also cover calls made from business numbers; the practical checklist is in [TRAI's new anti-spam rules](/blog/trai-anti-spam-rules-2026-businesses-that-call-or-message-customers). Bills sent by SMS should use a transactional template with your registered header.

## FAQ

### Can I create a bill in a mobile operator's name?

No. The tool is for billing in **your** business's name. Using an operator's name, logo or format to produce a document is forgery and can be prosecuted.

### What GST rate applies to telecom services?

18%. Use SAC 9984 on the invoice. Small operators under the composition scheme cannot charge GST separately, but telecom service providers are generally not eligible for composition anyway.

### How do I pro-rate a plan for a mid-month activation?

Monthly rental × (days active ÷ days in the cycle). Enter the activation date and the generator handles the arithmetic.

### Is a telecom bill valid as address proof?

Only bills from licensed telecom operators are accepted as address proof. A private bill from a reseller or employer is not.

### Can I add a late payment fee?

Yes. Add it as a one-time charge; it is part of the taxable value and attracts 18% GST when charged for a taxable service.
