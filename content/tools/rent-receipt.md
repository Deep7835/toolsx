If you claim House Rent Allowance, your employer will ask for rent receipts — usually one per month or one per quarter — before it exempts HRA from your salary under Section 10(13A). This generator prints correctly formatted rent receipts with the landlord's name, address and PAN, the period, the amount in words and a signature line, for a single month or a whole financial year in one PDF. It also works for commercial premises where a tenant business needs receipts for its own books.

## Why HRA claims fail without good receipts

HRA exemption is the smaller of three figures: actual HRA received, rent paid minus 10% of basic salary (plus DA), and 50% of basic in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata or Chennai (40% elsewhere). Employers compute this from the rent you declare, and Form 12BB asks you to attach proof. The receipts must be consistent — same landlord name, same address, sequential months — or payroll will simply exempt nothing and let you fight it out in your ITR. Work the numbers first with the [HRA exemption calculator](/tools/hra-exemption) and read [HRA exemption: the 3-way formula, rent receipts, landlord PAN](/blog/hra-exemption-rent-receipts-landlord-pan) for the edge cases.

Remember the exemption exists only under the **old tax regime**. If you have opted for the new regime, receipts still matter for your own records but do not reduce tax. Compare regimes in the [income tax calculator](/tools/income-tax).

## The landlord PAN rule and TDS

- If total rent in the year exceeds **₹1,00,000**, the employer must record the landlord's PAN (Circular 8/2013). If the landlord has no PAN, a signed declaration in the prescribed format is accepted.
- If **monthly rent exceeds ₹50,000**, you — the tenant — must deduct TDS under Section 194-IB at **2%** once a year (in the last month of the tenancy or of the financial year) and file Form 26QC. The [TDS calculator](/tools/tds-calculator) handles this section.
- Paying rent to parents is legal and common, but the money must actually move (bank transfer, not a notional entry), the parent should declare the rental income, and receipts should be issued like any landlord's.

## What a valid rent receipt contains

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Receipt number and date | Sequential; date it on or after the payment |
| Tenant name | As on the payslip |
| Landlord name, address, PAN | PAN needed above ₹1 lakh/year |
| Property address | Full address, including flat number |
| Period | "Rent for June 2026" or a quarter |
| Amount in figures and words | Matches your bank transfer |
| Mode of payment | UPI/NEFT reference preferred |
| Landlord signature | A ₹1 revenue stamp for cash above ₹5,000 is customary |

## How to use the generator

1. Enter tenant and landlord details, including PAN if rent exceeds ₹1 lakh a year.
2. Add the property address and the monthly rent. Choose **single month**, **quarter**, or **full financial year** — the yearly option produces 12 receipts, four to an A4 sheet.
3. Pick the payment mode and add the UPI or NEFT reference if you have it.
4. Download the PDF, get the landlord to sign, and upload it to your HR portal.

## Mistakes that trigger rejection

- **Round-number rent with no bank trail.** Payroll teams flag ₹8,333 cash receipts with no transfer record; pay by UPI and cite the reference.
- **Receipts all signed the same day with the same pen.** Better to generate them monthly, or at least date them correctly.
- **Wrong or missing PAN** above ₹1 lakh — the single most common reason HRA is denied.
- **Claiming HRA while living in your own house** or in a city where you also claim home-loan interest on the same property.
- **Landlord not reporting the income.** If the department cross-checks, the tenant's claim can be questioned.

## FAQ

### Is a rent receipt needed every month?

Employers accept monthly or quarterly receipts; some accept one consolidated annual receipt with a rent agreement. Monthly is safest because it matches your bank statement line by line.

### Do I need a rent agreement as well?

Not legally for HRA, but most employers ask for one when rent is high or when receipts are from a relative. A simple registered or notarised agreement removes doubt.

### Is a revenue stamp compulsory?

Only customary, for cash payments above ₹5,000. For UPI or bank transfers no stamp is needed; the transaction reference is stronger evidence.

### Can I claim HRA for rent paid to my parents?

Yes, if you genuinely pay them, they own the house, and they show the rent as income. Generate receipts in their name with their PAN.

### What if I forgot to submit receipts to my employer?

You can still claim the exemption in your income-tax return by recalculating salary income, provided you keep the receipts and bank proof in case of a notice.
