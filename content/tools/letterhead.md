A letterhead is the smallest piece of branding with the largest number of official uses: the declaration a bank wants "on company letterhead", the authorisation letter for a GST visit, the quotation cover, the NOC for a tenant, the experience certificate, the reply to a notice. This generator designs a **professional A4 letterhead** with your logo, legal name, address, GSTIN/CIN/Udyam number, contact details and footer, and lets you type the letter body and export a PDF — or download a blank template for Word and Google Docs. It runs in your browser; your details are saved locally for reuse.

## What belongs on a letterhead

| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| Logo and legal name | The registered name as on PAN/GST, with the trade name if different |
| Registered address, phone, email, website | The address on your GST certificate |
| GSTIN | Expected on letters to banks, vendors and government |
| CIN / LLPIN / Udyam number | Companies Act requires CIN, registered office and contact on all official letters; MSMEs benefit from showing Udyam |
| Footer | Bank details for vendor letters, or a one-line tagline; page number |
| Date, reference number, subject, salutation, signature block | The letter itself |

Under the Companies Act, a company must print its name, CIN, registered office address, phone, email and website (if any) on all business letters, billheads and official publications — a letterhead is the easiest way to comply.

## Signing letters

Printed and signed is still the norm; for PDFs, a signature image is widely accepted for commercial letters, while contracts, statutory filings and anything likely to be disputed deserve a DSC or Aadhaar eSign. The legal weight of each option is compared in [Digital signatures in India](/blog/digital-signature-dsc-vs-aadhaar-esign). Whatever you use, keep a dated copy in a "letters out" folder with its reference number.

## FAQ

### Is a letterhead legally required?

For companies, the Companies Act requires the name, CIN, registered office and contact details on business letters, which a letterhead satisfies. Proprietorships and partnerships are not compelled, but banks and government offices routinely ask for letters "on letterhead".

### Should I print GSTIN on the letterhead?

Yes, if registered. It saves being asked and signals a legitimate business. Include your Udyam number if you are a registered MSME.

### Can I use the PDF letterhead in Word?

Yes — export the blank template as DOCX, or insert the header image into your Word/Google Docs template.

### Is a scanned signature on a letterhead PDF valid?

For ordinary commercial correspondence, yes in practice. For contracts and statutory documents, use a DSC or eSign to avoid disputes.
