The offer letter is the first document a candidate ever receives from you, and the one they forward to their family, their current manager and sometimes a lawyer. A letter typed into a blank Word file — no letterhead, no salary breakup, a notice period that contradicts what the recruiter said — costs you candidates. This generator builds a complete offer letter on **your own letterhead**: upload a logo, enter the company details once, pick one of five templates, edit any line, and download a PDF with a CTC annexure and an acceptance block. Everything runs in your browser; no candidate's details are uploaded anywhere.

## What goes in the letter

**Your letterhead:** logo, company name, tagline, address, phone, email and website — entered once, stored in your browser, and reused by the [experience letter](/tools/experience-letter) and [internship certificate](/tools/internship-certificate) tools.

**The candidate:** name, postal address, designation, department, work location and reporting line.

**The offer:** reference number, date of issue, date of joining, and the date the offer lapses — the field most letters forget.

**The terms table:** designation, reporting line, place of work, joining date, CTC with its monthly equivalent, probation, notice, working hours and paid leave, in one scannable block before the numbered clauses.

## The salary annexure

Enter the annual CTC and what share of fixed pay is basic (40–50% is normal), and the annexure splits it:

| Component | How it is derived |
|---|---|
| Basic salary | Your chosen percentage of fixed pay |
| House rent allowance | 40% of basic |
| Employer PF | 12% of basic, capped at the ₹25,000 statutory wage ceiling |
| Gratuity provision | 4.81% of basic |
| Special allowance | The balance, so the components always total the CTC |
| Variable pay | Shown separately when you set a percentage |

It also prints the line that prevents the most common first-month argument: **cost to company is not take-home.** Employer PF, gratuity and variable pay are in the CTC and never reach the bank account, and employee PF, professional tax and TDS come out of gross. ## Getting the terms right

- **Notice period** must match what you will actually enforce. A 90-day notice on an offer letter is a 90-day notice when *they* leave; the arithmetic is in the [notice period calculator](/tools/notice-period).
- **Probation** of 3–6 months is standard. State explicitly that confirmation is in writing, or employees will argue it was automatic.
- **Basic at least 50% of wages.** Under the Labour Codes, "wages" must be at least half of total remuneration for PF and gratuity purposes, which limits the old trick of a tiny basic — see [The four Labour Codes are in force](/blog/labour-codes-2025-what-small-employers-must-change).
- **Offer validity** of 5–7 days is usual. Without it an offer stays open forever.
- **Conditional on verification**: keep the clause making the offer subject to documents, references and eligibility to work.
- **Statutory benefits** you must provide anyway — PF above 20 employees, ESI above 10 where wages are ≤ ₹21,000, gratuity after five years — do not need to be promised as perks, but the deductions should not surprise anyone.

## Offer letter, appointment letter, contract

An **offer letter** proposes the role and the terms, and lapses if not accepted. An **appointment letter** is issued on or after joining and is the employment contract proper, usually with fuller policy and termination clauses. Many small companies use one document for both, which is acceptable if it carries the full terms — this generator's letter does, with the acceptance block making it a signed agreement. ## FAQ

### Can I upload my company logo?

Yes. The logo goes into the letterhead at the top of both the letter and the annexure page, and is saved in your browser for the next letter and for the other Kaagazo document tools.

### Are the templates editable?

Fully. A template sets the opening paragraph, the numbered terms and the defaults for probation, notice and variable pay. Every one of those is a field you can rewrite before downloading.

### Is an offer letter legally binding?

Once the candidate signs and returns it, it forms a contract on the terms stated. That is why the validity date, the probation and the notice period need to be the real ones, not placeholders.

### Does it calculate the CTC breakup?

Yes. Enter the annual CTC and the basic percentage, and the annexure produces basic, HRA, special allowance, employer PF on the ₹25,000 ceiling, gratuity provision and any variable pay, totalling exactly to the CTC.

### Is the candidate's data stored anywhere?

No. The letter is built and rendered to PDF in your browser. Only your own company profile and logo are kept, in this device's local storage.

### What is the difference between an offer letter and an appointment letter?

The offer letter proposes the job and expires if not accepted; the appointment letter is issued at or after joining as the employment contract. A single document can do both if it carries the full terms and is signed, which is how this one is set up.
