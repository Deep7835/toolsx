Every wrong item shipped costs twice: the return shipping and the customer who does not come back. Order fulfilment accuracy — the share of orders picked, packed and delivered exactly as ordered — is the quality metric behind returns, refunds and reviews for any business that ships or delivers. This calculator computes **order accuracy and error rate** from your order counts, breaks errors down by type (wrong item, wrong quantity, damaged, missing, wrong address), and estimates what each error costs, so you know which fix pays first.

## The formula

Order accuracy = Orders fulfilled without error ÷ Total orders × 100.

A seller shipped 1,400 orders last month; 63 had an issue — 21 wrong item, 12 wrong quantity, 18 damaged, 7 missing item, 5 wrong address → accuracy **95.5%**, error rate 4.5%. If each error costs about ₹180 in reverse shipping, re-ship and handling, that is ₹11,300 a month before lost customers.

## How to use the calculator

1. Enter total orders and the number with errors (or enter errors by type).
2. Enter an average cost per error — reverse shipping plus re-shipment plus staff time — and optionally the refund/credit given.
3. Read accuracy, error rate, errors per 1,000 orders, and the monthly cost of errors by type.
4. Track weekly; add the previous periods to see the trend after a process change.
5. Combine with [on-time delivery](/tools/on-time-delivery) for the "perfect order" rate — on time, complete, undamaged, correctly invoiced.

## Where errors come from, and the fixes

| Error | Usual cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong item | Similar SKUs, no scan at pack | Barcode every SKU ([barcode generator](/tools/barcode-generator)) and scan-to-verify with the [scanner](/tools/barcode-scanner) |
| Wrong quantity | Counting by eye, bulk orders | Pick lists with quantities; weigh the packed parcel against expected weight |
| Damaged | Under-packing, wrong box | Right-size boxes; the [volumetric weight calculator](/tools/volumetric-weight) shows size trade-offs |
| Missing item | Multi-item orders packed in stages | Pack one order at a time; tick the list on the [shipping label](/tools/shipping-label) |
| Wrong address | Copy-paste from chats | Confirm PIN code and phone on WhatsApp before dispatch |
| Wrong invoice | Manual invoicing | Generate the [GST invoice](/tools/gst-invoice) from the order, not from memory |

Marketplaces track seller defect rates and suppress listings above thresholds (typically 1–2% for order defects); your own accuracy number will usually be the first warning.

## Benchmarks

Well-run small warehouses reach 99%+; 97–99% is common for small sellers packing by hand; below 95% signals a process problem rather than bad luck. Each point of accuracy on 1,000 orders is 10 fewer returns.

## FAQ

### What counts as an order error?

Any deviation from what was ordered — wrong item, wrong quantity, damage in transit, missing item, wrong address or invoice — reported by the customer or caught at dispatch.

### What is a good order accuracy rate?

Above 99% is the target for e-commerce; 97–99% is typical for hand-packed operations. Marketplaces start penalising sellers whose defect rates exceed about 1–2%.

### How do I estimate the cost of an error?

Reverse shipping (₹60–150), re-shipment, packaging, staff time, and any refund or discount. Add an estimate for lost repeat business if you want the full picture.

### Should I include courier damage?

Yes, in a separate category. It points to packaging or carrier choice rather than picking, and you may recover some of it from the carrier.

### How often should I measure?

Weekly for operations, monthly for trend reporting, and immediately after any change in staff, layout or packaging.
