Every "message us on WhatsApp" that makes a customer save your number first loses half of them. A **wa.me link** opens a chat with your business in one tap — no saving, no typing — and can carry a pre-filled message ("Hi, I'd like to order…") so the customer starts with the right words. This generator builds that link and its QR code for your website, Instagram bio, Google Business Profile, packaging and standees, with message templates for orders, quotes, bookings and support, entirely in your browser.

## How the link works

`https://wa.me/919876543210?text=Hi%20Kaagazo%2C%20I%20want%20to%20order` — the number in international format without plus or zeros, and the message URL-encoded. Tapping it on a phone opens WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business) to a chat with that number; on desktop it opens WhatsApp Web. The pre-filled text is editable by the customer before sending. The generator encodes the message correctly, handles emoji, and produces a QR that carries the same link for print.

## Templates that convert

- **Order**: "Hi! I'd like to order [item] × [qty]. Delivery to [area]."

- **Source tag**: add "(Ref: Insta)" or "(Ref: packaging)" at the end so you know which channel each chat came from — a free attribution system.

## How to use the generator

1. Enter your WhatsApp number with country code.
2. Pick a template or write your own message; the preview shows exactly what the customer sees.
3. Copy the short link for bios and websites, or download the QR as PNG/SVG for print. Choose a colour and add your logo label beneath the code.
4. Test on a phone before printing; make sure the number is the one running WhatsApp Business.

## FAQ

### Does the customer need to save my number?

No. The link opens the chat directly. That is the point — it removes the biggest drop-off in WhatsApp lead generation.

### Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes, with both the free WhatsApp Business app and the Business API. The link format is the same.

### Can I track clicks?

WhatsApp does not report clicks, but adding a source tag to the pre-filled message tells you which placement each conversation came from. Meta ads report click-to-WhatsApp conversions separately.

### Can I add a message with emoji or line breaks?

Yes. The generator URL-encodes emoji and line breaks correctly; the preview shows how it renders.

### Is a QR better than the link?

Use both: the link for digital placements, the QR for anything printed. The QR carries the same link and the same pre-filled message.
