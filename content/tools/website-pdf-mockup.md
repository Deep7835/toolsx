Web designers, agencies and freelancers spend hours turning screenshots into something a client can approve on a phone: a page per screen, the URL and title, a note on what changed, a place to comment. This tool lays out **website screenshots into a print-ready PDF presentation** — cover page, one or more screenshots per page with device frames, page titles and meta descriptions, annotations, and a closing page with your contact details — built in your browser from the images you drop in. Nothing is uploaded, and the output is a plain PDF that opens anywhere.

## What it is for

- **Design proposals and mockup reviews** — show desktop and mobile views side by side with a change list.
- **SEO audits** — pair each screenshot with the page's current title and meta description and your recommended versions from the [meta tag generator](/tools/meta-tags).
- **Handover documents** at project end — every page, its URL, and what was delivered, attached to the [GST invoice](/tools/gst-invoice).
- **Portfolio PDFs** for pitching new clients.
- **Compliance records** — a dated snapshot of your own website's privacy policy, terms and pricing pages as they appeared, useful for DPDP and consumer-law disputes.

## How to use the mockup builder

1. Drop screenshots (PNG/JPEG; full-page captures work best). Order them by drag.
2. For each, add the page title, URL, meta description and a note. Choose a frame — browser window, iPhone, Android, or none.
3. Pick a layout: one per page, two per page, or desktop + mobile pairs.
4. Add a cover (project name, client, date, your logo) and a closing page (contact, next steps, a [WhatsApp link](/tools/whatsapp-direct)).
5. Export PDF (A4 or 16:9 for screens). Compress images first with the [photo resizer](/tools/photo-resizer) if the deck gets heavy.

## Capturing good screenshots

- Use the browser's full-page capture (Chrome: DevTools → "Capture full size screenshot"; Firefox: right-click → Take Screenshot → Save full page).
- Capture at 1440 px width for desktop and 390 px for mobile emulation so proportions match real devices.
- Hide cookie banners and personal data before capturing; blur customer details.
- Capture the live [SERP preview](/tools/serp-preview) for SEO decks so the client sees before/after.

## Presenting a proposal that gets approved

- Lead with the client's goals and one screenshot per goal; do not show every page.
- Annotate what changes, not what stays.
- Show mobile first — most Indian traffic is mobile.
- End with scope, timeline and price, quoted on the [service quote builder](/tools/service-quote); ask for an advance against a [proforma invoice](/tools/proforma-invoice).
- Include your rate logic if challenged: the [freelance rate calculator](/tools/freelance-rate) makes it defensible.

## FAQ

### Does it take screenshots for me?

No. Browsers do not allow a web page to capture other sites. Capture with your browser's tools and drop the images in; the builder handles layout and export.

### Are my screenshots uploaded?

No. Images stay in your browser and the PDF is generated locally.

### Can I add my own branding?

Yes — logo, colours, cover and closing pages are all customisable and remembered for the next deck.

### What page size should I use?

A4 portrait for documents clients will print; 16:9 landscape for screen presentations and screen-share reviews.

### Can I include mobile and desktop views on one page?

Yes. Choose the paired layout; drop the desktop and mobile captures of the same page and they are placed side by side with device frames.
