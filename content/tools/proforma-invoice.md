A proforma invoice is the polite, precise way to say "this is what it will cost, and here is how to pay" before a sale is final. Exporters use it to open letters of credit, service firms use it to collect advances, and manufacturers use it to lock a quote into a document the buyer's finance team can process. This generator produces a clean, numbered proforma in your browser with your logo, GST breakup, bank details and an optional UPI QR — ready to send as a PDF or on WhatsApp.

## What a proforma invoice is (and is not)

A proforma is a **commitment to sell at stated terms**, issued before goods are dispatched or services delivered. It is not a tax invoice: it does not create a GST liability, cannot be used by the buyer to claim input tax credit, and does not enter your GSTR-1. Once the buyer accepts and pays (or the goods move), you issue the actual [GST tax invoice](/tools/gst-invoice), which should mirror the proforma's line items so the two reconcile.

Because it looks like an invoice, label it clearly — the generator prints "PROFORMA INVOICE" in the header and a footer note that it is not a demand for tax. Give it its own numbering series (PI-2609-0001, for instance) so it never collides with your tax-invoice numbers.

## When Indian businesses use one

- **Advance payment requests.** Interior contractors, event planners, and custom manufacturers ask for 30–50% upfront against a proforma. When the advance arrives, issue a [payment receipt](/tools/payment-receipt); GST on advances for goods is not payable until supply, while advances for services are taxable when received.
- **Exports and imports.** Banks need a proforma to process LC applications and outward remittances; customs brokers use it to pre-classify HSN codes.
- **Corporate buyers.** Many companies cannot raise a purchase order without a proforma from the vendor. Match their [purchase order](/tools/purchase-order) to your proforma line by line.
- **Government tenders and institutional sales**, where a "quotation-cum-proforma" is the expected format.

If you only need to state a price without payment instructions, a [service quote or estimate](/tools/service-quote) is lighter. The differences are covered in [Invoice vs bill vs receipt vs quotation](/blog/invoice-vs-bill-vs-receipt-vs-quotation).

## What to include

| Section | Details |
|---|---|
| Header | "Proforma Invoice", your legal name, address, GSTIN, logo |
| Numbering | Unique series and date; validity date (e.g. 15 days) |
| Buyer | Name, billing and shipping address, GSTIN if registered |
| Items | Description, HSN/SAC, quantity, unit, rate, discount, taxable value |
| Tax | CGST + SGST or IGST by slab; total in words |
| Terms | Advance %, delivery lead time, payment mode, bank account and IFSC, UPI ID |
| Sign-off | Authorised signatory, "This is not a tax invoice" note |

## Practical tips

- **Validity dates protect you** from steel, copper or fuel price swings. Thirty days is normal; seven for volatile inputs.
- **Quote inclusive of GST for consumers, exclusive for businesses.** B2B buyers reclaim GST and want the taxable value visible.
- **State delivery terms** ("ex-works Ludhiana", "door delivery Mumbai, freight extra") to avoid arguments about who pays transport.
- **Number sequentially** and store PDFs; auditors sometimes ask to see the proforma behind a large advance.

## FAQ

### Does a proforma invoice attract GST?

No. GST becomes payable on the tax invoice or, for services, on receipt of an advance — whichever is earlier. The proforma itself is not a supply document, so it carries no tax liability and does not go into GSTR-1.

### Can the buyer claim ITC on a proforma?

No. Input tax credit requires a valid tax invoice under Section 16. That is why the proforma must clearly say it is not a tax invoice.

### Should the proforma and final invoice have the same number?

No. Keep separate series. The tax invoice can reference the proforma ("Against PI-2609-0007") for easy matching.

### Is a proforma legally binding?

It is an offer. Once the buyer accepts — usually by paying the advance or sending a PO — the terms on it become the contract terms, so write delivery, validity and payment conditions carefully.
