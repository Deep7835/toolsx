From **15 October 2026**, UPI is no longer free for every merchant. Under the NPCI circular of 15 September 2026, person-to-merchant (P2M) payments above ₹2,000 carry a merchant discount rate of **0.4%, capped at ₹300 per transaction**; fuel, railways and telecom pay a flat ₹5; and small merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh a month through UPI QR (P2PM) pay nothing. This calculator turns those rules into a monthly rupee figure for your shop — by category, ticket size and volume — including the 18% GST on the fee and the input credit you can claim back.

## The rules the calculator applies

| Merchant category | MDR from 15 October 2026 |
|---|---|
| P2PM small merchant (UPI QR receipts ≤ ₹1 lakh/month) | Nil, on every transaction |
| P2M merchant, transaction ≤ ₹2,000 | Nil |
| P2M merchant, transaction > ₹2,000 | 0.4% of the amount, capped at ₹300 (cap reached at ₹75,000) |
| Fuel, railways, telecom (essential category), > ₹2,000 | Flat ₹5 |
| GST | 18% on the MDR, claimable as ITC if your GSTIN is on the PSP account |

Consumers pay nothing extra, and merchants **may not add a surcharge** for UPI. What the circular says, who is exempt and how petrol pumps and traders reacted is in [UPI MDR starts 15 October: what the NPCI circular actually says](/blog/upi-mdr-from-15-october-npci-circular-explained).

## How to use the calculator

1. Pick your category: P2PM, P2M, essential (flat ₹5), or custom if your PSP has quoted a different rate or cap.
2. Enter a typical transaction amount and the number of transactions per month above ₹2,000.
3. Read the MDR per transaction, the monthly fee, the GST on it, the ITC you recover, and the **effective cost as a percentage of UPI sales**.
4. Change the ticket size to see where the ₹300 cap starts to help (above ₹75,000 the effective rate falls below 0.4%).
5. Use the figure in the [profit margin calculator](/tools/profit-margin) as a per-unit payment cost.

## Worked examples

- **Kirana, average bill ₹600**: almost every transaction is below ₹2,000 and receipts are under ₹1 lakh a month → **₹0**.
- **Boutique, average bill ₹4,500, 300 bills a month above ₹2,000**: 0.4% × 4,500 = ₹18 per bill + ₹3.24 GST → about ₹6,400 a month, of which ₹970 comes back as ITC. Effective cost ≈ 0.47% of those sales.
- **Electronics dealer, ₹85,000 average**: MDR capped at ₹300 (0.35%) + ₹54 GST per sale.
- **Petrol pump, ₹3,000 fill**: flat ₹5 + ₹0.90 GST — 0.2%, well below card MDR.

## What to do before 15 October

1. **Confirm your tag with your PSP** (PhonePe, Paytm, BharatPe, your bank). Receipts under ₹1 lakh a month should be P2PM with zero MDR; get it in writing.
2. **Put your GSTIN on the PSP account** so the monthly MDR invoice supports ITC.
3. **Reprice, do not surcharge.** If high-ticket margins are thin, revise the price list — a "2% extra on UPI" board breaks the rules and loses sales.
4. **Keep the standee up.** UPI remains cheaper than cards (1–2%) and far cheaper than cash handling; refresh yours with the [UPI QR standee](/tools/upi-standee).
5. **Reconcile monthly**: MDR is deducted from settlements; match the PSP statement to your [payment receipts](/tools/payment-receipt).

## FAQ

### Does UPI MDR apply to my small shop?

Only if you receive more than ₹1 lakh a month via UPI QR (P2M status) *and* the transaction is above ₹2,000. Most kiranas, stalls and small service providers stay at zero.

### Can I charge customers 0.4% extra for UPI?

No. NPCI rules prohibit passing MDR on to customers, and the Finance Ministry has said it will act against surcharges. Build the cost into pricing instead.

### Is the ₹300 cap per transaction or per day?

Per transaction. A ₹1 lakh payment attracts ₹300, not ₹400.

### Is GST on MDR recoverable?

Yes, as input tax credit, provided your GSTIN is on the PSP account and the PSP issues a GST invoice for the fee.

### Do payments to a personal VPA attract MDR?

No. Person-to-person transfers remain free. Only merchant-tagged QRs, payment links and gateways are P2M.
