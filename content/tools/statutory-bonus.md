The Diwali bonus is not a gift for many workers — it is a statutory right. The Payment of Bonus Act, 1965 requires every factory and every establishment with 20 or more employees to pay an annual bonus of **at least 8.33% and up to 20%** of wages to employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month, computed on a wage ceiling of ₹7,000 or the minimum wage, whichever is higher. This calculator works out the bonus per employee for a full or part year, the minimum and maximum, and the total liability for your team, so you budget it before the accounts close.

## The rules in brief

| Point | Rule |
|---|---|
| Coverage | Factories, and establishments with 20+ employees (once covered, always covered) |
| Eligible employee | Wages up to ₹21,000 a month; at least 30 working days in the year |
| Calculation ceiling | Bonus computed on ₹7,000 a month or the state minimum wage for the job, whichever is higher |
| Minimum bonus | 8.33% of the calculation wages (even in a loss year) |
| Maximum bonus | 20%, depending on "allocable surplus" — profits after the statutory formula |
| Payment deadline | Within 8 months of the close of the accounting year (by 30 November for a March year end) |
| Deductions | Customary or interim bonus already paid; misconduct-related loss with due process |
| Exemptions | New establishments for the first 5 years unless they earn profits; LIC, certain public bodies |

Start-ups are exempt only until they make a profit in any of the first five years. Employees dismissed for fraud or violence lose entitlement for that year.

## How to use the calculator

1. Enter the employee's monthly wages (basic + DA) and the state minimum wage for the category, if higher than ₹7,000.
2. Enter days worked in the year — bonus is pro-rated for part years; below 30 days there is no entitlement.
3. Choose the bonus percentage (8.33% minimum; up to 20% if your allocable surplus allows) or enter your declared rate.
4. Read the bonus payable and, in team mode, the total for all eligible employees; export the list for payroll.
5. Pay through payroll — bonus is taxable salary, subject to TDS — and record it on the [wage slip](/tools/wage-slip).

## Worked example

A packer earns ₹15,000 a month, worked the full year, minimum wage for the category is ₹11,500. Calculation wage = ₹11,500 (higher than ₹7,000). Minimum bonus = 8.33% × 11,500 × 12 = **₹11,495**. At 20%, ₹27,600. An office assistant at ₹22,000 a month is outside the Act entirely — any bonus is contractual, not statutory.

## Bonus, ex-gratia and "Diwali gift"

Many employers pay everyone something at Diwali; the statutory bonus is the floor for covered employees, and anything for those above ₹21,000 is **ex-gratia** at the company's discretion. Keep the two separate in policy and on payslips. Productivity-linked bonus schemes may replace the statutory bonus only with an agreement and only within the 8.33%–20% band. Employers must also file the annual return in Form D and maintain registers A, B and C.

## FAQ

### Is bonus mandatory for a shop with 12 employees?

Not under the Payment of Bonus Act, which applies to establishments with 20 or more employees (and all factories). Some state rules and contracts may still require it; check your Shops and Establishments Act.

### Who is eligible?

Employees earning wages up to ₹21,000 a month who have worked at least 30 days in the accounting year. Apprentices under the Apprentices Act are excluded.

### What if the company made a loss?

The minimum 8.33% is still payable, except for new establishments in their first five years without profit.

### Is the bonus taxable?

Yes, it is salary income and subject to TDS in the month of payment.

### When must it be paid?

Within eight months of the end of the accounting year — by 30 November for a 31 March year end — unless the government grants an extension.
