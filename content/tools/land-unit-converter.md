A bigha in Uttar Pradesh is not a bigha in West Bengal, a katha in Bihar is nearly twice a katha in Kolkata, and "gaj" means square yards to a Delhi broker and something vaguer to everyone else. Land deals, agricultural leases, property tax notices and Google Maps measurements all speak different units, and the conversion mistakes cost lakhs. This converter translates between **square feet, square metres, square yards (gaj), acres, hectares, bigha, biswa, katha, dhur, guntha, cent, ground, kanal and marla**, with state-specific definitions for the traditional units, entirely in your browser.

## Where conversions matter

- **Agricultural deals**: quoted per bigha or acre; loan eligibility and crop insurance use hectares.
- **Plots and flats**: builders quote super built-up in sq ft; RERA carpet area is in sq m; stamp duty circle rates are per sq m or per sq yd.
- **Property tax and building plans**: municipal FSI/FAR calculations run in sq m.
- **Commercial leases**: rent per sq ft per month; the [rent receipt](/tools/rent-receipt) and [GST invoice](/tools/gst-invoice) for commercial rent need the area only for reference, but disputes start with it.
- **Registration**: the deed must state the area in the unit used in revenue records, usually with a metric equivalent.

## Tips

- Ask sellers "which bigha?" and get the sq ft or sq m equivalent in writing.
- Measure large plots with a total station or DGPS survey; phone GPS errs by 3–10 m.
- For carpet vs built-up vs super built-up, expect loading of 20–35% in apartments; convert all three before comparing per-sq-ft rates.
- Circle-rate calculators on state registration portals use sq m — convert first, then check stamp duty.

## FAQ

### How many square feet in one bigha?

It depends on the state: 27,225 sq ft in Uttar Pradesh (pucca), Bihar and Rajasthan (pucca); 14,400 in West Bengal and Assam; 17,424 in Gujarat; 9,070 in Punjab and Haryana. Select the state in the converter.

### How many bigha in an acre?

About 1.6 in UP/Bihar (27,225 sq ft bigha), 3.025 in West Bengal, 2.5 in Gujarat and 4.8 in Punjab/Haryana.

### What is a gaj?

A gaj is a square yard — 9 square feet, or 0.836 square metres. Plots in Delhi, Haryana and Rajasthan are commonly quoted in gaj.

### How many cents in an acre?

100. One cent is 435.6 sq ft, used in Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Karnataka.

### Is the converter accurate for legal documents?

The standard units are exact. Traditional units follow the most common state definition; verify against the revenue record for the specific village before signing.
