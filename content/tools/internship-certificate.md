Interns collect certificates the way employees collect payslips: colleges demand them for credit, placement cells verify them, and the intern's first job interview will ask what the project was. A good internship certificate names the intern, the role, the exact dates, the project or area of work, and who supervised it — on letterhead, signed. This maker formats **internship and training completion certificates** for companies, startups, CA firms, hospitals, NGOs and colleges, with a project title, skills, a performance line and a verification number, exported as PDF or PNG from your browser.

## What a college or employer expects to see

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Organisation name, logo, address, registration or CIN | Verifies the issuer exists |
| Certificate number and date of issue | Placement cells cross-check by number |
| Intern's full name, college and programme | Matches the college's records |
| Role or department | "Marketing intern", "Articled assistant", "Software development trainee" |
| Period with start and end dates, and total duration | "2 June 2026 to 29 August 2026 (13 weeks)" |
| Project title and 1–2 line description | The most useful sentence for the intern |
| Skills or tools used | Optional: "Python, Power BI, GST return preparation" |
| Performance line | "completed the internship satisfactorily" or a stronger statement if earned |
| Stipend statement (optional) | "This was a paid internship" — needed by some universities |
| Signatory | Supervisor or HR head, with designation and signature |

## Rules worth knowing

- **Stipend and tax**: stipends are generally taxable as salary or "income from other sources"; TDS applies only if the amount crosses the basic exemption limit. Many companies pay stipends through payroll — the [team payroll calculator](/tools/team-payroll) handles part-month pro-rating.
- **Apprentices vs interns**: apprentices under the Apprentices Act have a prescribed minimum stipend and a registered contract; ordinary interns do not, but paying a fair stipend is expected by top colleges.
- **Letters for AICTE/NEP internships**: universities may require the certificate to state hours completed and a mentor's contact — the template includes both fields.
- **Working hours and safety** rules of the Shops and Establishments Act apply to interns as to staff.
- If the intern joins full-time later, issue a separate [experience letter](/tools/experience-letter) for the employment period.

## Making the certificate useful for the intern

- Name the **project**, not just the department. "Built a GST reconciliation sheet used by the accounts team" beats "worked in finance".
- List tools honestly; recruiters test them.
- Include the mentor's designation and a way to verify — email on the letterhead, or a QR from the [URL QR generator](/tools/url-qr) pointing to a verification page.
- Send a PDF **and** a printed copy; colleges still file paper.

## FAQ

### Is an internship certificate legally required?

No statute mandates it, but colleges require it for credit and it is standard practice. Issue one at the end of every internship, even unpaid ones.

### Should the certificate mention the stipend?

Only if the intern or their college asks. A line such as "This was a paid internship with a monthly stipend" is enough; the amount need not be stated.

### Can I issue it for a virtual internship?

Yes. State "remote/virtual" in the mode of work and include the hours or deliverables so the college accepts it.

### What is the difference between an internship certificate and a training certificate?

Training certificates cover structured programmes (a course with a syllabus); internship certificates cover work experience under supervision. The maker has wording for both.

### Is a digital signature acceptable?

Yes. Most colleges accept PDFs with a scanned or digital signature on official letterhead, and many verify by emailing the address on it.
