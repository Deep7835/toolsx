The to-do list that runs a small business is not an app with 40 features — it is the sheet on the counter that says what must happen today, who does it, and what is done. This generator builds **clean, printable task lists and checklists** with priorities, due dates, assignees and completion tracking, exportable as PDF for the wall or PNG for the team WhatsApp group, with a completion ratio that updates as you tick items. Lists are saved in your browser, not on a server.

## What it does

- **Task lists** with priority (high/medium/low), due date, owner and notes.
- **Recurring checklists** — opening and closing routines, weekly stock checks, month-end compliance — that reset each cycle.
- **Completion ratio** and progress bar for each list; a summary of overdue items.
- **Print layouts**: A4 checklist with tick boxes, A5 daily sheet, or a compact PNG for sharing.
- **Templates** for common business routines (below).

## How to use the generator

1. Start from a template or a blank list; name it and set a cycle (once, daily, weekly, monthly).
2. Add tasks with priority, due date and owner; drag to order.
3. Tick tasks as done; the ratio and overdue count update. Recurring lists reset on schedule and keep the history.
4. Print the A4/A5 layout or share the PNG; export CSV for a spreadsheet.
5. Keep it short — a list with more than 15 items is a project plan, and belongs in two lists.

## Running the day with a list

- Write tomorrow's list at closing time, not in the morning.
- Three "must" items at the top; everything else is "if time".
- Assign every task to a person; unassigned tasks are undone tasks.
- Review the completion ratio weekly; a list that is always 60% done is over-stuffed or under-staffed — the [capacity utilization](/tools/capacity-utilization) tool gives the numbers.
- Put compliance dates on the calendar as well as the list; the list is for the day, the calendar for the month.

## FAQ

### Where are my lists stored?

In your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Export CSV or PDF as a backup, because clearing site data removes them.

### Can my team share a list?

Share the PNG or PDF; the tool itself is single-device. For live collaboration use a shared document and keep this for printing and routines.

### Can I set recurring tasks?

Yes — daily, weekly or monthly checklists reset automatically and keep a completion history.

### Is there a limit on tasks?

No hard limit, but lists print best under 25 items. Split long lists by area or owner.

### Can I print tick-box sheets?

Yes. The A4 and A5 layouts include tick boxes, priorities and a signature line for the person completing the routine.
