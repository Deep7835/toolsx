Most business PDFs — quotes, invoices, letters, certificates — are signed by pasting an image of a signature. Done well, that image is a crisp, transparent PNG that looks like ink; done badly, it is a phone photo of a signature on lined paper with a grey box around it. This maker lets you **draw a signature with your finger or mouse, or type your name in signature-style fonts**, cleans it up, and exports a transparent PNG (plus SVG) at print resolution for use in every document tool on Kaagazo and in Word, Docs, Canva and PDF editors. It runs in your browser; the signature never leaves your device.

## Image signature, DSC and Aadhaar eSign: what is valid for what

- **Signature image**: accepted in practice for commercial correspondence, quotations, invoices, certificates and most internal HR documents. It is easy to copy, so it proves intent weakly if disputed.
- **Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)**: a USB-token-based signature recognised under the IT Act, mandatory for company filings (MCA), GST returns of companies and LLPs, income-tax audit reports and e-tenders.
- **Aadhaar eSign**: OTP-based electronic signature, legally valid under the IT Act, used for loan agreements, rental agreements and many fintech flows.

Use an image for speed on everyday documents, and a DSC or eSign for contracts, statutory filings and anything a court might see. The legal detail is in [Digital signatures in India: DSC vs Aadhaar eSign vs a signature image](/blog/digital-signature-dsc-vs-aadhaar-esign).

## How to use the maker

1. **Draw**: sign on the pad with a finger, stylus or mouse; adjust stroke width and smoothing. **Type**: enter your name and pick a style.
2. Choose ink colour (blue or black look most authentic), then trim and clean the background — the tool removes stray marks.
3. Export PNG (transparent, 300 dpi) and SVG; a 600 px-wide PNG suits A4 documents.
4. Insert into the [letterhead](/tools/letterhead), [service quote](/tools/service-quote), [experience letter](/tools/experience-letter), [award certificate](/tools/award-certificate) or any PDF editor.
5. Store the file somewhere private; treat it like a stamp.

## Getting a natural-looking signature

- Sign larger than you normally would; it scales down cleanly.
- A slight blue ink and 2–3 px stroke reads as a pen; pure black at 1 px looks digital.
- Keep a version with your name typed beneath for certificates and letters.
- Do not add a date to the image; type the date in the document.

## Using it responsibly

- Never sign blank letterheads or send your signature file to anyone.
- For invoices, GST rules accept a digital signature or no signature at all on e-invoices; a signature image is fine on printed invoices from the [GST invoice generator](/tools/gst-invoice).
- Cheques and bank forms need a wet signature matching the bank's record; do not print an image on them.
- For signatures by staff on your behalf, issue a written authorisation on letterhead.

## FAQ

### Is a signature image legally valid?

For everyday commercial documents it is widely accepted and can evidence intent, but it is not a "digital signature" under the IT Act. Use a DSC or Aadhaar eSign where the law requires one or the stakes are high.

### Is my signature uploaded anywhere?

No. Drawing, cleaning and export happen in your browser; the file is saved only where you save it.

### PNG or SVG?

PNG with transparency for inserting into documents and PDFs; SVG for designers and for scaling without blur.

### Can I use it on GST invoices?

Yes, on printed or PDF invoices. E-invoices reported to the IRP carry the portal's digital signature and need no separate signature.

### Can I make a signature for a company stamp?

Yes — draw or type the company name in a rounded style, export PNG, and use it beside the signature; a physical rubber stamp remains customary for banks and government offices.
