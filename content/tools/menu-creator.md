Printed menus go out of date the week onion prices spike. A digital menu lives at a link you control, updates in seconds, and reaches the customer through a QR on the table — no app to install, no aggregator commission. This tool lets you build a categorised menu with prices, veg/non-veg markers, descriptions and daily specials, generates the QR for table tents and the door, and includes a camera QR scanner so you can test it on the spot. Everything is created in your browser and the menu link is shareable on WhatsApp and Instagram.

## Who this is for

Cafés, dhabas, cloud kitchens, tiffin services, bakeries, bars, and hotel room-service desks — anyone who changes prices or dishes more often than they want to reprint. It also suits salons and clinics that publish a "menu" of services, and home bakers who send a WhatsApp catalogue every festive season. Restaurants competing with delivery apps are using QR menus plus a [WhatsApp order link](/tools/whatsapp-direct) to take direct orders without the 20–30% commission; see [Quick commerce vs the kirana](/blog/quick-commerce-vs-kirana-how-to-compete) for how neighbourhood businesses are winning direct customers back.

## What a good digital menu includes

| Element | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Categories in eating order | Starters → mains → breads → desserts → drinks; customers scan top to bottom |
| Veg / non-veg / egg markers | Mandatory under FSSAI labelling norms for packaged food and expected on menus |
| Prices with "taxes extra" or "inclusive" stated | GST on restaurant food is 5% without ITC; state it once, clearly |
| Short descriptions and allergens | "Contains nuts", "spicy", "Jain option available" reduce back-and-forth |
| Specials and out-of-stock toggles | Hide the paneer tikka the moment it runs out |
| Contact and order link | Phone, WhatsApp, delivery radius, timings |

Menu items are a good place to reuse product photos — compress them with the [photo resizer](/tools/photo-resizer) so the page loads on slow mobile data, and clean the background with the [background remover](/tools/background-remover) for a consistent look.

## How to use the menu creator

1. Add your outlet name, logo, timings and contact.
2. Create categories, then items with price, veg/non-veg flag and an optional description or photo. Drag to reorder.
3. Mark today's specials and hide anything unavailable.
4. Publish the menu link and download the **QR** — print it on table tents, the counter, the door and takeaway boxes.
5. Use the built-in **camera scanner** to test the QR from a phone before printing 50 copies.

## Pricing and GST on the menu

Restaurant service is taxed at **5% GST without input credit** for standalone restaurants, and 18% with ITC for those inside hotels with room tariffs above ₹7,500. Most small outlets print inclusive prices for walk-ins and add "5% GST extra" for billed orders — whichever you choose, say it on the menu and match it on the bill from the [GST invoice generator](/tools/gst-invoice). If you are under the composition scheme (turnover up to ₹1.5 crore, 5% for restaurants), you cannot charge GST separately at all; the [GST composition scheme guide](/blog/gst-composition-scheme-2026-complete-guide) explains the trade-off.

Use the [profit margin calculator](/tools/profit-margin) to price dishes on food cost — most cafés aim for 30–35% food cost — and the [discount calculator](/tools/discount-calculator) for happy-hour or combo pricing.

## FAQ

### Do customers need an app to view the menu?

No. Scanning the QR opens a web page in the phone's browser. It works on any smartphone with a camera app that reads QR codes, which is every phone sold in the last several years.

### Can I take orders through the menu?

The menu links to your WhatsApp with a pre-filled message, so customers can order directly. It is not a full ordering system with payments; pair it with a UPI QR for that.

### Is a digital menu allowed under FSSAI rules?

Yes. FSSAI requires veg/non-veg symbols and, for licensed outlets, the licence number displayed; you can add it in the footer text of the menu.

### How do I update prices?

Edit the item and save; the QR does not change because it points to the same link. Reprint nothing.

### Does the built-in scanner store what it scans?

No. It runs in your browser using the device camera and shows the decoded text on screen only.
