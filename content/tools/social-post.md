The hard part of social media for a small business is not the photo — it is the 40 words under it, five times a week. This generator drafts **ready-to-post captions** for Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Status, LinkedIn and X from a few inputs: what you are promoting, the offer, the call to action and your tone. It formats them for each platform (line breaks, emoji density, hashtag sets, character limits), adds your WhatsApp or website link, and lets you copy the result in one tap. Drafts are generated in your browser from structured templates; nothing is uploaded.

## What a post needs

| Element | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Hook (first line) | The only line shown before "more"; a question, a number, or the offer |
| Body | What it is, who it is for, why now — two or three short lines |
| Proof | A detail: "made fresh at 6 am", "300+ five-star reviews", "GST invoice included" |
| Call to action | One action: order on WhatsApp, visit, DM, save the post |
| Link or handle | wa.me link, website, location tag |
| Hashtags | 5–10 relevant ones on Instagram, 1–3 on LinkedIn, none on WhatsApp |

## How to use the generator

1. Choose the platform and post type — product launch, offer, festive greeting, behind-the-scenes, testimonial, hiring, announcement.
2. Enter the product or service, the offer or key detail, your location and your call to action; pick a tone (friendly, premium, playful, formal) and language (English, Hindi, Hinglish).
3. Read the three draft variations; pick one and edit.
4. Add your [wa.me link](/tools/whatsapp-direct) or website; the tool checks length limits and suggests a hashtag set.
5. Copy and post; save the template for the next one.

## Platform notes

- **Instagram**: 2,200-character limit but the first 125 matter; put the CTA early; 5–10 niche hashtags beat 30 generic ones; add location.
- **Facebook**: longer text works; link previews matter; local groups are where small businesses get reach.
- **WhatsApp Status and broadcast**: short, image-first, price visible; broadcasts need opt-in — see [WhatsApp Business per-message pricing](/blog/whatsapp-business-per-message-pricing-2026).
- **LinkedIn**: for B2B suppliers and services — a short story, one insight, no hard sell; 3 hashtags.
- **X**: under 280 characters; one image; reply to your own post with the link.

## Compliance in captions

- Offers must state conditions ("on bills above ₹999, till 31 Oct") and match the [price tags](/tools/price-tag-generator) in store.
- Do not use "best", "no. 1" or health claims you cannot substantiate; ASCI guidelines apply to social ads, and FSSAI rules to food claims.
- Testimonials need the customer's permission; mark paid partnerships as such.
- Festive posts: check dates; Diwali, Eid and Pongal move each year.

## FAQ

### Are the captions generated by AI on a server?

Drafts are assembled locally in your browser from structured templates and your inputs — nothing is sent to a server. Edit them to sound like you.

### How many hashtags should I use?

Instagram: 5–10 specific to your product and city. LinkedIn: 1–3. Facebook and WhatsApp: none or one.

### Can it write in Hindi?

Yes — English, Hindi and Hinglish (Roman script) are supported for all templates.

### How often should a small business post?

Three to five times a week on one primary platform beats daily posting on four. Consistency and replies matter more than volume.

### Do I need to mark offers with terms?

Yes. Consumer protection and ASCI guidelines require material conditions to be disclosed; the generator prompts you for validity dates and minimum bill.
