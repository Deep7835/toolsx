The Code on Wages requires every employer to give workers a wage slip — and small shops, godowns, restaurants and workshops are exactly where slips go missing. A slip protects both sides: the worker has proof of earnings for a loan or a rent agreement, and you have a signed record when a labour inspector or a dispute asks how ₹14,600 was arrived at. This generator prints **thermal (80 mm) or A5 wage slips** for daily-rated and monthly-paid staff, with overtime, bonus, advances, PF/ESI and net pay, in a minute, without uploading anything.

## What a wage slip must show

| Line | Detail |
|---|---|
| Employer name and address; worker name, designation, employee ID | Identification |
| Wage period and payment date | "1–30 September 2026, paid 5 October" |
| Days worked / paid days; daily rate or monthly wage | Basis of pay |
| Earnings | Basic, DA, HRA, overtime (hours × rate), bonus, incentives |
| Deductions | Employee PF (12%), ESI (0.75%), professional tax, advances recovered, fines within legal limits |
| Net wages paid and mode | Cash, UPI, bank; UTR if digital |
| Signatures | Employer/authorised person and worker acknowledgement |

Minimum wages are set by the state for each schedule of employment and skill level; the slip should show a rate at or above the applicable minimum, with DA revisions in April and October in most states.

## The statutory pieces

- **EPF** applies to establishments with 20 or more employees (many register voluntarily below that). Employee and employer each contribute 12% of basic + DA, now on wages up to the **₹25,000 ceiling** effective 17 September 2026 (raised from ₹15,000); what this means for take-home and cost is in [EPF wage ceiling is now ₹25,000](/blog/epf-wage-ceiling-25000-what-changes-for-payroll).
- **ESI** applies to establishments with 10 or more employees where gross wages are up to ₹21,000: employee 0.75%, employer 3.25%.
- **Overtime** is paid at twice the ordinary rate under the Factories Act and most shop and establishment acts.
- **Statutory bonus** of 8.33%–20% is due to workers earning up to ₹21,000 who have worked 30 days in the year; the [statutory bonus calculator](/tools/statutory-bonus) works it out.
- **Deductions** are capped — fines under 3% of wages, and total deductions generally under 50% of wages under the Code on Wages.

The four Labour Codes brought in the "50% wages" definition that affects PF and gratuity; what small employers must change is summarised in [The four Labour Codes are in force](/blog/labour-codes-2025-what-small-employers-must-change).

## Paying wages correctly

- Pay by the **7th** of the next month (10th for establishments with over 1,000 workers) under the Code on Wages.
- Prefer UPI or bank transfer — it is your proof, and cash wages above ₹10,000 per person per day are disallowed as an expense under Section 40A(3).
- Keep a wage register and the signed slips for at least three years; labour inspectors ask for both.
- When a worker leaves, settle within two working days under the Code; the [notice period calculator](/tools/notice-period) helps with the last-day arithmetic.

## FAQ

### Is a wage slip mandatory for daily-wage workers?

Yes. The Code on Wages requires a wage slip for every employee, including casual and daily-rated workers, at or before payment.

### What is the minimum information on a slip?

Employer and worker identification, wage period, rate and days, each component of earnings and deductions, and net wages paid. The template covers all of them.

### Do I have to deduct PF for a worker earning ₹12,000?

If your establishment is covered under EPF (20+ employees, or voluntarily registered), yes — PF applies to all employees with wages up to the ₹25,000 ceiling, and above it for existing members.

### Can I recover an advance from wages?

Yes, in instalments, with the amount shown on the slip. Total deductions should stay within the limits of the Code on Wages (generally 50% of wages).

### Can I print on a normal printer?

Yes. Choose the A5 layout; two slips fit on an A4 sheet. The 80 mm layout is for thermal receipt printers.
