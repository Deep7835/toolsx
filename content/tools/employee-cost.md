A ₹40,000-a-month hire costs more than ₹4.8 lakh a year. Add employer PF, ESI, gratuity provision, statutory bonus, insurance, equipment and the seat they occupy, and the "fully-loaded" cost lands closer to ₹5.8–6.5 lakh. Small businesses that price projects, bid for contracts or decide between a hire and a freelancer need that number, not the payslip figure. This calculator builds the **total annual employee cost** from gross salary and your statutory obligations, with the 2026 EPF ceiling, ESI limits and bonus rules applied automatically.

## The components

| Item | Rule (2026) |
|---|---|
| Gross salary | Basic + HRA + allowances, monthly × 12 |
| Employer EPF | 12% of basic + DA, on wages up to the **₹25,000 ceiling** (or full basic if you opt), plus EDLI 0.5% and admin charges 0.5% |
| Employer ESI | 3.25% of gross where gross ≤ ₹21,000 (establishments with 10+ employees) |
| Gratuity provision | 4.81% of basic + DA, payable after 5 years (pro-rata for fixed-term staff after 1 year) |
| Statutory bonus | 8.33%–20% of ₹7,000 or minimum wage, whichever is higher, for employees earning up to ₹21,000 |
| Leave encashment provision | Optional; days × per-day rate |
| Insurance, meals, transport, phone | Actuals |
| Equipment, software seats, workspace | Amortised per employee |
| Recruitment and training | One-time, spread over expected tenure |

The [EPF ceiling change](/blog/epf-wage-ceiling-25000-what-changes-for-payroll) on 17 September 2026 raised the employer's PF outgo for anyone with basic between ₹15,000 and ₹25,000 by up to ₹1,200 a month; the calculator reflects it.

## How to use the calculator

1. Enter monthly gross and the basic percentage (40–50% is typical).
2. Toggle the statutory items that apply: EPF (mandatory at 20+ employees), ESI (10+ employees, gross ≤ ₹21,000), gratuity and bonus.
3. Add benefits and overheads — insurance premium, meals, laptop, software, desk.
4. Read the annual cost, the monthly loaded cost and the **cost per working hour** (based on 8-hour days and your working-day calendar) for pricing.
5. Compare against a contractor's quote using the [freelance rate calculator](/tools/freelance-rate) from the other side.

## Using the number

- **Pricing services**: a consultant costing ₹1,200 an hour loaded must bill at ₹2,000–2,400 to cover utilisation of 60% and margin. The [profit margin calculator](/tools/profit-margin) closes the loop.
- **Hire vs outsource**: compare the loaded cost with a vendor's annual fee, remembering that GST on a vendor's invoice is creditable while payroll costs are not.
- **Budgeting**: multiply loaded cost by headcount for the year's people budget; add the increment percentage.
- **Structure**: the Labour Codes require basic (wages) to be at least 50% of total remuneration for PF and gratuity purposes, which limits the old trick of a tiny basic. Details in [The four Labour Codes are in force](/blog/labour-codes-2025-what-small-employers-must-change).

## Common omissions

- Forgetting **EDLI and admin charges** (about 1% on PF wages).
- Ignoring **gratuity** because "nobody stays five years" — it is a real liability that auditors expect provisioned.
- Missing the **bonus** for staff up to ₹21,000, which is payable within eight months of the year end.
- Not counting **paid leave** — 24 days of leave on a 300-day year is 8% of cost.
- Treating **reimbursements** as free — fuel and phone bills are still cash out.

## FAQ

### What percentage should I add to salary for statutory costs?

Roughly 13–20% of gross for employees within the PF and ESI limits (PF 13%, ESI 3.25%, gratuity 4.81% of basic, bonus), and 8–13% for higher earners outside ESI and bonus coverage.

### Is employer PF part of CTC?

Usually, yes — companies include it in the offered CTC. This calculator starts from gross, so add employer PF as a cost rather than deducting it from CTC.

### Do I need to pay ESI for an employee earning ₹25,000?

No. ESI applies only where gross wages are ₹21,000 or less (₹25,000 for persons with disabilities).

### Is gratuity a cost even in year one?

Accounting-wise, yes: provide 4.81% of basic each year so the liability is funded when the employee crosses five years. Fixed-term employees earn it pro-rata after one year.

### How do I compute hourly cost?

Annual loaded cost ÷ (working days × 8). The calculator uses 250 working days by default; adjust for your calendar.
