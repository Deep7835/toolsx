"No refunds" written on a bill is not a policy; it is an invitation to a chargeback, a one-star review and a consumer complaint. A clear refund and return policy — what can be returned, within how many days, in what condition, who pays shipping, when the money comes back and how — is required for online sellers under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules and is the cheapest way any shop, agency or app reduces disputes. This generator drafts a **refund, return and cancellation policy** for physical products, services, digital goods and subscriptions, tuned to how you actually operate, ready to publish.

## What a compliant policy states

| Item | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Return window | 7, 10, 15 or 30 days from delivery; state the trigger date |
| Eligible items and conditions | Unused, original packaging, tags intact; exclusions (perishables, customised, innerwear, digital downloads once accessed) |
| Process | How to initiate (WhatsApp, email, form), what to include (order ID, photos), pickup vs self-ship |
| Refund method and timeline | To the original payment mode within X working days after receipt/inspection; store credit options |
| Shipping costs | Who pays for returns; free for damaged/wrong items |
| Cancellations | Before dispatch free; after dispatch as a return; service cancellations with notice periods |
| Exchanges | Size/colour swaps, subject to stock |
| Damaged or wrong items | Report within 48 hours with photos; replacement or full refund including shipping |
| Non-refundable cases | Clearly listed and reasonable |
| Contact and grievance | Named contact, response time |

## How to use the generator

1. Choose what you sell — physical goods, services, digital products, subscriptions, or a mix.
2. Set the window, conditions, who pays return shipping, refund method and timeline, and any exclusions from the checklist.
3. Add cancellation rules (orders and appointments) and exchange rules.
4. Generate, adjust wording, and publish at `/refund-policy`; link from checkout, the [terms](/tools/terms-generator), order confirmations and your marketplace store page.
5. Keep the same rules on WhatsApp orders — paste the short version in a quick reply.

## Rules and realities in India

- **E-commerce sellers must disclose** return, refund, exchange, warranty and cancellation terms up front; a "no returns" blanket policy on defective goods is an unfair trade practice.
- **Marketplaces** (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho) impose their own return windows — your policy applies to your own channels but should not promise less than the law.
- **GST**: refunds after invoicing need a [credit note](/tools/credit-note); returns reduce your outward supply in the month of the credit note.
- **Payment gateways** expect a published policy; chargeback disputes are decided largely on whether you followed it.
- **Services**: stage-wise cancellation charges must be proportionate; advances taken on a [proforma invoice](/tools/proforma-invoice) should state what is refundable.
- The [Consumer Protection E-Commerce Rules and DPDP checklist](/blog/dpdp-rules-2025-checklist-for-small-websites) covers the disclosure and data side together.

## Running returns without losing money

- Photos and size charts prevent most apparel returns; the [background remover](/tools/background-remover) and [photo resizer](/tools/photo-resizer) help.
- Inspect before refunding; refund within the promised time — late refunds are what customers complain about, not the policy itself.
- Track return rate in the [seller pricing workflow](/tools/seller-pricing) and [order accuracy calculator](/tools/order-accuracy); a rising rate is a product or description problem.
- Offer store credit with a small bonus as an option, never as the only option.
- Log every return with reason; the [CSAT calculator](/tools/csat-calculator) shows whether handling is working.

## FAQ

### Is a refund policy legally required?

For online sellers, yes — the E-Commerce Rules require disclosure of return, refund, exchange and cancellation terms. For physical shops it is not mandated, but a displayed policy is your protection under consumer law.

### Can I say "no refunds"?

For customised, perishable or hygiene-sensitive goods and for services already rendered, restrictions are reasonable. A blanket refusal for defective or wrong items is an unfair trade practice.

### How fast must I refund?

State a timeline and keep it — 5–7 working days after receiving the return is standard; gateways may take a few more days to credit.

### Do I need a credit note for a refund?

If a GST invoice was issued, yes. Issue a credit note under Section 34 referencing the invoice so your tax liability is adjusted.

### Do marketplace rules override my policy?

On the marketplace, its rules apply to that order. Your own policy governs your website, WhatsApp and in-store sales.
