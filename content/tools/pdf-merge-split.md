The CA wants the invoice, the e-way bill and the transporter's receipt as one PDF. The bank wants the last six months of statements in one file under 5 MB. A customer wants only page 3 of the catalogue. A tender needs the certificates in a specific order. This tool **merges PDFs, splits them into pages or ranges, reorders and rotates pages, and compresses the result** — entirely in your browser. Files never leave your device, which is exactly what you want for documents that contain GSTINs, bank statements and PAN cards.

## What it does

- **Merge**: combine several PDFs (and images — JPEG/PNG are converted to pages) in the order you set.
- **Split**: extract a page range, every page as a separate file, or split at fixed intervals.
- **Reorder and delete**: drag pages, remove blanks, rotate scans that came in sideways.
- **Compress**: downsample images inside the PDF to hit upload limits.
- **Page numbers and a simple header/footer**: for compiled submissions.

## Everyday uses

- **GST and audit packs**: invoice + [delivery challan](/tools/delivery-challan) + e-way bill + LR per consignment; monthly [GST invoices](/tools/gst-invoice) into one file for the accountant.
- **Loan applications**: ITRs, bank statements, GST returns and Udyam certificate in the lender's required order.
- **Tenders and vendor registration**: PAN, GST certificate, cancelled cheque, MSME certificate, [letterhead](/tools/letterhead) declaration — one PDF, numbered.
- **HR files**: offer letter, [experience letters](/tools/experience-letter), ID proofs per employee.
- **Catalogues and proposals**: the [website mockup deck](/tools/website-pdf-mockup) plus the [service quote](/tools/service-quote) as a single send.
- **Customer documents**: a single page from a 40-page manual or policy.

## How to use it

1. Drop files. Thumbnails appear; drag to reorder, click to rotate or delete.
2. Choose **Merge** for one output, or **Split** and enter ranges (e.g. `1-3, 7, 10-12`) or "every page".
3. Optionally add page numbers and compress (choose a target quality).
4. Download. Large merges of scanned pages may take a few seconds — processing happens on your device.

## Scans, signatures and size

- Phone scans come in as large JPEGs; compress after merging to stay under portal limits (many accept 2–5 MB).
- For signed documents, add a signature image from the [digital signature maker](/tools/digital-signature) before merging; for legally strong signatures use a DSC — the differences are in [Digital signatures in India](/blog/digital-signature-dsc-vs-aadhaar-esign).
- Text in scans is not searchable; extract it with the [OCR tool](/tools/image-ocr) if you need to copy figures.
- Keep the originals; a merged file is a convenience copy.

## Privacy

Bank statements, PAN, Aadhaar and GST documents are exactly what should never pass through a random online PDF site. This tool uses a PDF library running in your browser; there is no upload, no account and no retention. Under the DPDP Act you are responsible for how customer documents are handled — local processing is the simplest way to stay on the right side of it (see [DPDP Rules 2025 for small businesses](/blog/dpdp-rules-2025-checklist-for-small-websites)).

## FAQ

### Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. Merging, splitting and compression run in your browser. Close the tab and nothing remains.

### Is there a file size or page limit?

Practical limits depend on your device's memory; files up to a few hundred pages work on most phones and any laptop.

### Can I merge images with PDFs?

Yes. JPEG and PNG files become pages sized to A4 (or their own aspect ratio if you prefer).

### Can I password-protect the output?

Yes — set an open password when downloading. Remember that password protection is not encryption at rest for the recipient; share the password separately.

### Will compression reduce quality?

It downsamples embedded images; text stays sharp. Choose a moderate setting for documents that will be printed or verified.
