Recognition is the cheapest retention tool a business has, and a well-designed certificate makes it real: "Employee of the Quarter", "Best Sales Team — Diwali 2026", "Ten Years of Service", "Excellence in Customer Care". This designer produces print-ready **award and merit certificates** with your logo, the recipient's name, the citation, date, signatories and an optional border and seal, exported as high-resolution PNG or A4 PDF in landscape or portrait. It works for companies, schools, coaching institutes, NGOs, sports clubs and events, and runs entirely in your browser.

## Certificate anatomy

| Element | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Title | "Certificate of Excellence", "Award of Merit", "Appreciation" — 3–5 words |
| Presented to | The recipient's full name, largest text after the title |
| Citation | One or two lines saying **what** they did: "for achieving 142% of the Q2 target while mentoring two new joiners" |
| Issuer | Company or institution name and logo |
| Date and place | Prevents the "when was this?" problem years later |
| Signatories | One or two: MD, HR head, principal, club president |
| Serial number / QR (optional) | For verification — link the QR to a page or record using the [URL QR generator](/tools/url-qr) |

## How to use the designer

1. Choose a template — classic bordered, modern minimal, or bold colour block — and orientation.
2. Enter the title, recipient, citation, date and issuer; upload a logo and, optionally, a signature image from the [digital signature maker](/tools/digital-signature).
3. Pick the colour theme to match your brand and adjust the font pairing.
4. For a batch, paste a list of names — the tool generates one certificate per name with the same design.
5. Download PNG for email and social media, or PDF for printing on 250–300 gsm ivory or linen card.

## Making recognition count

- **Be specific.** "For outstanding performance" is forgettable; "for zero delivery errors across 1,200 orders in the festive season" is repeated at family dinners.
- **Be timely.** Give the certificate within days of the achievement, in front of the team.
- **Pair it with something tangible** — a voucher, a day off, a mention on the company's social page (draft it with the [social media post generator](/tools/social-post)).
- **Track it.** A simple register of awards helps appraisals and shows patterns of who is never recognised.
- **Tax note**: gifts and awards from an employer are tax-free up to ₹5,000 a year in aggregate; above that the value is taxed as a perquisite. Cash awards are salary.

## Print specifications

- A4 landscape (297 × 210 mm) is the standard frame size; the PDF includes 3 mm bleed for professional printing.
- Use 300 dpi PNG for print; the designer exports at that resolution.
- Ivory, cream or linen-textured card between 250 and 300 gsm gives the right feel; avoid glossy photo paper.
- Gold foil or embossed seals can be added by any local printer — leave the seal area blank in the design if you plan to.

Recognition works best when it is part of a fair, compliant workplace — the employer-side changes under the new labour framework are summarised in [The four Labour Codes are in force](/blog/labour-codes-2025-what-small-employers-must-change).

## FAQ

### Can I generate certificates for a whole list of people?

Yes. Batch mode takes a list of names (one per line) and produces a PDF with one certificate per page, all in the same design.

### What size should I print?

A4 is standard and fits common frames. The PDF is set up with bleed so a printer can trim it cleanly.

### Are employee awards taxable?

Non-cash gifts and awards up to ₹5,000 per year are exempt as a perquisite; the value above that is taxable in the employee's hands. Cash awards are treated as salary.

### Can I add a verification QR?

Yes — generate a QR to a verification page or a Google Sheet record and place it in the corner. Serial numbers on each certificate make lookups simple.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Names, logos and signatures stay in your browser; the certificate image is rendered locally.
