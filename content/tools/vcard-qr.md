A visiting card that has to be typed into a phone is a visiting card that ends up in a drawer. A **vCard QR** puts your name, company, designation, phone numbers, email, website and address into a single code: the customer scans it with the camera, taps "Add contact", and you are in their phone — spelled correctly, with the right country code. This generator builds the standard vCard 3.0 QR that iPhone and Android read natively, with a preview of exactly what the contact card will show, and exports PNG or SVG for cards, standees, email signatures and slide decks.

## What goes into the vCard

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Name (first, last) and organisation | The contact's display name |
| Title / designation | "Proprietor", "Sales Manager" |
| Mobile and work phone | International format (+91…) so it dials from anywhere |
| WhatsApp number | Saved as a phone; pair with a [wa.me link](/tools/whatsapp-direct) for one-tap chat |
| Email and website | Website with https:// |
| Address | Shop or office; opens in Maps from the contact |
| Note | GSTIN, working hours, or a one-line pitch |

Keep it lean: every extra field enlarges the QR and makes it harder to scan at small sizes. Name, company, one mobile, email and website is the sweet spot.

## How to use the generator

1. Fill in the fields; the preview updates as you type.
2. Choose QR size, colour and error-correction level (M is the default; use H if you plan to place a small logo in the centre).
3. Download PNG for print or SVG for designers, or copy the vCard text to attach as a `.vcf` file in emails.
4. Test with an iPhone and an Android before printing — both should show "Add to contacts".

## Where businesses use vCard QRs

- **Visiting cards**: the QR replaces the back-of-card clutter; keep the front minimal.
- **Shop signage and standees**: next to the [UPI QR](/tools/upi-standee) so customers save your number for reorders.
- **Email signatures** and PDF quotes from the [service quote builder](/tools/service-quote).
- **Exhibition stalls and conference badges**: dozens of scans a day with zero typing errors.
- **Field sales and delivery staff**: on the [staff ID card](/tools/staff-id-card) back, so customers can call the right person.
- **Property and vehicle listings**: a QR on a "For sale" board.

## vCard vs "link-in-bio" pages

A vCard QR works offline and requires no page to load; a link page (Linktree-style) lets you update details after printing and add more links. Many businesses use both: vCard QR on the card, a [URL QR](/tools/url-qr) to the profile page on the standee. If you change your number, a printed vCard QR is stale; a link page is not.

## Privacy and practical notes

- The QR contains the data itself; anyone who scans it has the details, so print only what you want public — a business mobile, not a personal one.
- Nothing is uploaded: the vCard is generated and rendered in your browser.
- Under the DPDP Act, collecting *customers'* contacts (for example by scanning their cards) needs a stated purpose and their consent; the checklist is in [DPDP Rules 2025 for small businesses](/blog/dpdp-rules-2025-checklist-for-small-websites).
- Use the same name format as your Google Business Profile so saved contacts and search results match.

## FAQ

### Does the vCard QR work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Both camera apps recognise vCard data and offer to add the contact without any third-party app.

### Can I include a photo?

Photos make the QR far too dense to scan reliably. Leave the photo out; the customer can add one later.

### What if my phone number changes?

A printed vCard QR cannot be updated. Reprint, or use a URL QR to a page you control for details that may change.

### Is my data sent to a server?

No. The vCard text and the QR image are created entirely in your browser.

### What size should the QR be on a visiting card?

At least 18–20 mm square with a clear margin. With a full vCard the code is dense, so avoid going smaller than 15 mm.
