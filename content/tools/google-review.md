Reviews decide which shop the map shows first. A business with 120 reviews at 4.6 stars gets the call; the one with 9 reviews at 5.0 does not. The difference is rarely quality — it is asking. This builder creates a **polite review request** with a direct link to your Google Business Profile's "write a review" form, ready to send on WhatsApp or SMS or print as a QR for the counter, table or delivery bag, so asking becomes a habit instead of an afterthought.

## Getting your review link

Google gives every verified Business Profile a short link of the form `https://g.page/r/XXXX/review`. Find it in the Business Profile dashboard under "Ask for reviews" (or "Get more reviews"). Paste it here; the tool validates the format. If you only have your Place ID, the generator builds the `search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=…` form of the link, which opens the same review box.

## What a good request looks like

- **Short and personal**: "Thanks for visiting Sharma Electricals today, Ravi! If we did well, a Google review would help us a lot: [link]."
- **Timed**: within a few hours of delivery or the visit, while the experience is fresh.
- **Easy**: one tap to the form; no "search for us on Google".
- **Honest**: never offer discounts or gifts for reviews — Google's policies prohibit incentives, and Consumer Protection rules on misleading endorsements apply.
- **Follow-through**: reply to every review, especially negative ones, within a day.

The builder offers templates for retail, restaurants, clinics, services and home delivery, in English and Hindi, with your business name and the customer's name merged in.

## How to use the builder

1. Paste your Google review link (or Place ID).
2. Pick a template and adjust the wording; add the customer's name if sending individually.
3. Tap **Share on WhatsApp** to send it, or copy the message for SMS and email.
4. Download the **QR code** with a "Rate us on Google" caption for the counter, the bill, or the packaging.
5. Save the message as your default; the tool remembers it in your browser.

## FAQ

### Where do I find my Google review link?

In Google Business Profile: open your profile from Google Search or Maps as the owner, choose "Ask for reviews", and copy the short link. The builder accepts that link or your Place ID.

### Can I offer a discount for a review?

No. Google's policies prohibit incentivised reviews and may remove them or penalise the profile; Indian consumer-protection guidelines on endorsements also apply.

### Can I ask for only positive reviews?

Ask everyone; do not filter customers by satisfaction before showing the link ("review gating"), which Google prohibits.

### How do I respond to a negative review?

Reply within a day, thank them, acknowledge the specific issue, say what you have changed, and invite them back or to contact you directly. Never argue in public.

### Does the QR work if my profile link changes?

Google's short review links are stable; if you re-verify or merge profiles, regenerate the QR with the new link.
