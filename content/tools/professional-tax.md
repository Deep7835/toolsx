Professional tax is the small monthly deduction on the payslip that confuses new employees and new employers alike: it is a **state** tax, the slabs differ from Maharashtra to West Bengal, the annual ceiling is ₹2,500, and several states do not levy it at all. This calculator gives the monthly PT for a salary in Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu and Madhya Pradesh, applies the February adjustment where a state uses one, and shows the employer's annual outgo per employee.

## How professional tax works

Article 276 of the Constitution lets states tax professions, trades and employment, capped at **₹2,500 per person per year**. Employers deduct it from salary each month and deposit it with the state's commercial-tax department; self-employed professionals and businesses pay their own enrolment tax annually. The deduction reduces taxable salary under Section 16(iii) — but only under the old regime. Some states (Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Uttarakhand) do not levy PT; others (Bihar, Odisha, Assam, Jharkhand, Kerala, Meghalaya, Sikkim, Tripura, Manipur, Nagaland, Mizoram, Puducherry) levy it on their own slabs.

## How to use the calculator

1. Pick the state where the employee **works** (not where the company is registered).
2. Enter monthly gross salary; for Maharashtra, choose gender because women earning up to ₹25,000 are exempt.
3. Read the monthly deduction, the February or year-end adjustment, and the annual total.
4. For payroll, feed the figure into the [in-hand salary calculator](/tools/in-hand-salary) or the [team payroll calculator](/tools/team-payroll), which apply PT by state automatically.

## Employer obligations

- **Registration**: a Professional Tax Registration Certificate (PTRC) to deduct from employees and a Professional Tax Enrolment Certificate (PTEC) for the business itself, usually within 30 days of employing staff.
- **Deposit and returns**: monthly in most states (Maharashtra: monthly if annual liability ≥ ₹1,00,000, otherwise annual); Karnataka by the 20th; Tamil Nadu half-yearly.
- **Penalties**: interest around 1.25–2% per month plus fixed penalties for late registration or non-deduction.
- Show PT as a separate line on the payslip; the [wage slip generator](/tools/wage-slip) has a field for it.

## Notes on gross salary

PT slabs apply to gross salary or wages for the month, generally including basic, DA, HRA and allowances but excluding reimbursements and employer PF. Bonus paid in a month can push that month's PT into a higher slab in slab-based states. A new joiner who works part of a month is assessed on actual salary paid for that month.

## FAQ

### Is professional tax deductible from income tax?

Yes, under Section 16(iii), but only if you are on the old regime. The new regime does not allow it.

### Who pays PT for a proprietor or freelancer?

You pay enrolment tax yourself, annually, at the flat rate your state prescribes (₹2,500 in Maharashtra and Karnataka for most professions).

### My company is in Bengaluru but I work from Delhi. Which state applies?

PT depends on where the employee works. Delhi levies none, so no PT is due, though many payrolls default to the company's state — worth correcting.

### Why is my February deduction higher?

Maharashtra collects ₹300 in February (instead of ₹200) so the annual total reaches the ₹2,500 ceiling. Madhya Pradesh has a similar year-end top-up.

### Can PT exceed ₹2,500 a year?

No. Article 276 caps it at ₹2,500 per person per year; states set their slabs within that ceiling.
